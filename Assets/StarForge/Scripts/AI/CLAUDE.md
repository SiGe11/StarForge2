# AI (Commander, MechBrain) — area notes

Root `CLAUDE.md` holds the cross-cutting rules; this file holds the AI lessons. Mech rules/physics: `../World/Mech/CLAUDE.md`.

## Pipeline

`GameBootstrap.StartMatch` builds a `Commander` for team 1 (and one for team 0 when spectating), ticked from `GameWorld.Ticked`.
`Perception` (fog-limited memory) → `OpponentModel` (Bayesian filter, six player styles) → `StrategySelector` (contextual bandit, eight plans) → `Commander` (macro, scouting, tactics, micro).
`AIMemory` persists bandit weights and the player profile to `persistentDataPath/starforge_ai_memory.json` and seeds the next match's priors.

**Invariants for any Commander change:** enemy state only through `GameWorld.Visible`/`VisibleCell`, never `units`; every order through the public `GameWorld.Cmd*` the mouse uses, paid from `ActionBudget` (APM token bucket) and delayed by the per-difficulty reaction time.
**MechBrain is exempt by design:** omniscient, ~1,200 APM. The Commander learns about the Mech only through its advice.
**Perception keeps one memory per sighting** (each takes the nearest entry of its type no other sighting took this pass, `claimed`): taking the first entry within 7 m folded a parked squad into one or two, so a raid read as a scout and home defence never diverted. **The influence map counts only finished armed enemy structures** (a Sentinel, a Mech Bay's gun; a site has no gun). Check `BugTrials.Sight`: every Trooper of a 3.4 m clump that is in sight remembered (9 of 9, 10 of 10), still so five passes later; threat at a bay site 0.00, the finished bay 3.99 (read through `ComputeCPU`: the GPU backend answers a frame or two late).

**Variety.** `Commander.DrawPersonality` draws a `Personality` from the match seed (opening, unit-mix shifts, push timing, flank/second-prong taste, per-plan score offsets), avoiding the last two learned openings (`AIMemoryData.recentOpenings/recentPlans/recentWins`). `StrategySelector.SetBias` nudges away from recent plans; a plan two minutes without paying off grows stale. Waves (`StartWave/RunWave/EndWave`) pick target and approach from the influence map, stage, may split a prong. Memory off (benchmark, evaluation): only the seed varies it.

**AI internals are developer-only.** Anything exposing the AI's beliefs/plans, or letting a player disable/reset its memory, is gated on `MatchSettings.debugAI` (`-sfdebug` or the editor menu). Player-facing text: `HUDController.OpponentHelp` and the README "The opponent AI" section; keep both consistent with `Commander`.

**The memory file is not trusted** (`AIMemory.Sanitize`): non-finite weights, histogram or plan times are dropped (the doctrine stands in), the profile is clamped to 0-1 (non-finite to its default), wins to the games played; and `StrategySelector.Reward` skips a non-finite reward or update. A NaN weight passes `Mathf.Clamp` and every later update, and is saved again: from a hand-edited or corrupted file the AI's choice of plan was broken in every match after. Check `BugTrials.MemoryFile` (edit mode; the real file copied aside and put back): NaN weights, NaN histogram, a NaN and a 1e39 profile, 9 wins in 5 games, Infinity plan times, bad history indices, truncated, garbage, empty: before, the NaN file loaded 160 non-finite weights and an out-of-range profile and the selector built from it had non-finite weights; now every case finite and in range (unreadable files start fresh, as before).
**Statics persist across play sessions (domain reload off).** Trials silence the opponent with `Commander.Suspended`; one left on made a quick evaluation lose 3 of 4 to an AI that never moved. `AIEvalRunner`/`BenchmarkRunner` clear it; a one-off `Unity_RunCommand` trial must too. `Commander.IgnoreSites`, `Commander.MechGateInBase`, `Commander.RepositionOff` (the blocked-shot trial's, which the runners did not reset), `MechCore.LegacyBalance` and `GameWorld.LegacyBreakPick` are the same kind of A/B switch (runners reset them). **`MatchSettings.aiMemory` too:** trials (BugTrials, MechTrials, FxLook, GroundLook) start their matches with memory off and none put it back, so `BugTrials.ClearOnPlay` turns it on again at every play entry outside the debug view (players cannot turn it off; the toggle is debug-only), and `AIEvalRunner` puts it back the same way (probe `BugTrials.EvalAbortState`: off during a trial, on at the next plain Play).

## Mech AI

**The Mech takes no orders from its side**: every player `Cmd*` skips `def.Autonomous`. Its AI (`MechBrain.cs`, one per side, ticked by `GameBootstrap`) drives it only through `CmdMechMove`/`CmdMechFocus`/`CmdMechHalt`. The player's Mech speaks through `MechAdvise` (`GameEventKind.MechAdvice`): the HUD shows it as comms, and a Commander on that side (spectating) hears it in `OnWorldEvent`. The opponent's Mech calls its Commander directly (below). Either way `Commander.HearMech` heeds or ignores by `Personality.adviceTrust`.

**Two minds, one judgement** (`MechDoctrine.cs`; the user's call): both Mechs fight the same cautious way -- more defending than attacking, going in when the moment calls for it -- but the opponent's is its own mind, not a copy, and not a different strategy. Team 0's keeps MechBrain's original numbers (`Guardian()`); team 1's draws a temper per match from the seed (`Tempered`: steady, wary or bold, then +-4% jitter, then **every number clamped to the band**: repair 0.34-0.42, odds x0.92-1.1, hunt from 0.68-0.78, reach 135-165 m, worth 135-170, guard 20-24 m, and their Mech never taken on at under 0.92 of the player's Mech's odds -- caution and the duel factor multiply, and unclamped a bold one went in at 0.83). An aggressive "hunter" doctrine was designed and rejected: do not make the opponent's Mech bolder than this band. Only repair (also where `BaySafe` sends a hurt Mech home), caution, duel, the hunt gate, hunt reach/worth and the guard post read the doctrine; the logic of `BaySafe`, `HomeSide`, the bay shun, `HomeThreat`, `thinkEvery` and `Punish` does not. `MechBrain.Debug` (replay trace, AI inspector) starts with the doctrine name; the per-match evaluation line does not print it. Gate (Quick, 2026-10-07): 2/4 wins, 0 losses; the AI Mech's time stayed cautious (vs Macro: guarding 25%, defending 9%, supporting 15%, hunting 21%, withdrawing 12%, repairing 13%); BayEdgeCases case 0: bay in its base down in 101 s, 0 fighting units lost.
**Only the player's Mech talks.** Team 1's `Say` raises no event: its Attack/Defend/Regroup go straight to `Commander.HearMech` (`MechBrain.Listener`, set by `GameBootstrap`; a missing listener logs one warning rather than going unheard), the same kind and place the event carried; its news, status (deserter verdicts included) and morale are not sent at all, and `Speak` returns before raising anything. It keeps a MechSpeech, whose mood and temper weigh `Punish`, but never speaks or writes the journal. `Say`'s pacing and counts are the same on both sides. `MechTrials.Watch` therefore logs only team 0's comms; team 1's calls show as its Commander's heeded/ignored (Quick: heard in 3 of 4 games). Real news is `Status` (REPORT), never `Morale`: their Mech down, their bay down.

**Speech** (`MechSpeech.cs`): 406 morale lines tagged by situation and tone, chosen from a reading of the battle about once a second: standing 0.7 armies-and-Mechs / 0.3 bases (on all assets it read "even" through a match one side was losing); fights tracked by area, closed after 15 s with no death within 60 m (one map-wide feed never went quiet); turning points, extremis, near defeat, calm global or local to a guarding Mech; weights and cues. Own `Rng`; speaks through `MechBrain.Speak`, which must **not** push `nextAdvice` (speech must never delay the calls the Commander acts on). Kept rare: never within 30 s of any comms line, 90 s between its own, at most 4 in ten minutes.
Checks via `editor.sh call`: `MechSpeech.Check` (all there, filled in, every bucket reachable), `MechSpeech.JournalReport` (the player's Mech only: calls shown, repeats kept off, flavour lines and lines a minute, then every line and what it noticed). A reading with no eligible line spends no slot (it retries in 10 s).
**Comms**: a call about the same place within 150 s is `GameEvent.repeat` (a 1.6x bigger incoming force is news): HUD and radio drop it (its map ping is refreshed, not added), a Commander hearing the event still gets it. The HUD tags calls (SUGGESTION/WARNING/REPORT) and shows flavour (Morale) quoted and muted (`comms-line--flair`), with a softer radio chirp, never pushing a call off the feed. Speech yields to the calls: never within 30 s of any comms line, 90 s between its own lines, at most 4 in ten minutes, fight outcomes only for fights worth 600+. Checked in a frozen match: a repeat adds no line; a call at a full feed evicts the flavour, not a call; flavour at a feed full of calls is dropped.

**Punishing deserters** (`MechBrain.Punish`, rules in `../World/CLAUDE.md` Morale): on its side's `Desertion` event it **decides 4-9 s later, whatever it is doing, and says so** (deciding only once free -- 60% hull, out of a fight, the base safe -- a Mech whose side was losing its war never was: 3 of 3 desertions with a Mech on the field got no verdict in a Full eval). will = 0.12 + 0.4 coldness - 0.1 warmth + 0.15 pride + 0.35 bad mood (`MechSpeech.Mood`, which draws no random number so it cannot shift speech), clamped 0.03-0.9, rolled on its own `punishRng`. A "go" stands until the deserters are gone; it **goes only while free** (60%+ hull, not in a fight, no `HomeThreat` of 120+, their Mech not within 60 m) for `FreeFor` 3 s, and turns back if called away once set off (`pursuing`), saying so only the first time (`turnedBack`: sniped on its way it said it every few seconds). Owing them a visit at under 60% and otherwise free (`FreeForDeserters`, the same test as going: on "nothing within 34 m" it walked off to mend while shelled from 40 m), it **tops up at its bay**, saying so (its repair line is lower, and nothing else mends it: it waited on them all match); never free for `GoLimit` 300 s since it last went after them, it lets them be with a `NoTime` line. A Mech still coming down at the break gives no verdict. The purge is on only while it is after them: `Punish` sets it when going and clears it when busy, and `Update` clears it when repairs or a hull under its repair line take over (those branches return before `Punish`); a dead Mech ends it, and so does the match's end (`Update` returned on the winner before clearing it, and its guns kept on at them behind the end screen; `BugTrials.PurgeEnd`: on, then off 2 s after a surrender). It gives up after `PursuitLimit` 150 s of actual pursuit (`TimedOut` line). Focus the nearest, stand where most guns reach a rifleman (`PunishStand`: a mortar cannot fire inside 10 m). "It is done" only if it put one down itself (`executedAtGo`), else "the enemy found them first". **Narration goes through `Narrate`: Status (Morale when it spares them), never Attack/Defend/Regroup** -- team 1's Commander acts on those and would send its army to its own corner -- and, unlike `Say`, does not push `nextAdvice` (team 1's counts nothing: it says nothing); team 1's Mech decides the same way, silently. `PunishNow()` forces a "go" for trials. **The evaluation reseeds the verdict rolls per opponent** (`ReseedPunish`, from `AIEvalRunner` beside `ReseedMorale`): from the seed alone the first verdict of each of its five seeds was the same against all four opponents (team 1's first rolls 0.36/0.61/0.86/0.74/0.68, so seeds 2-5 could never punish); `BugTrials.PunishSeeds` prints both. **A draw** (winner 2) gets no final line from the Mech, prints "draw" on the evaluation line, and is a game not won in `AIMemory` (`games` up, `aiWins` not, `recentWins` 0). `LastVerdict` records the will, the roll's inputs and, sparing them, whether it said so and its roll (five silent spares in a row once looked like a bug; they were a 1% streak). A run takes the Mech 50-90 m from its post: the enemy Commander may read that as `MechAway`. Trials (World notes): decided at 50% hull and said so, deserters untouched for 20 s, all 4 down once mended (`PunishHurt`); decided in the gantry and went once mended (`PunishRepairing`); topped up at 50% and went (`PunishTopUp`), held while hit from 45 m and topped up after (`PunishTopUpUnderFire`); no bay, gave up at 300 s (`PunishNeverFree`); its own verdict 4-9 s after the break (`PunishNatural`), none from a Mech coming down (`PunishInbound`); unforced cold verdicts (will 0.30-0.54) put 4 down by themselves. **In a Full eval every desertion with a Mech on the field got a verdict (4 of 4: 2 punish, 2 spare), and one punishment put its deserter down itself** (Turtle game 1); the other "go" never found a free moment before its Mech fell 289 s later. Without a Mech the deserters mostly died to the enemy within a minute.

**Commander vs the enemy Mech.**
- While the enemy Mech is about, the Commander waits for a bigger army before pushing (`Plan`: x1.5 with its own Mech, x1.9 without, +20% per wave that Mech beat back, `mechRepulses`, up to four). Before the last factor it fed a defending Mech one wave at a time and matches ran to the cap.
- It plays against the Mech it has seen: `Perception` reads its guns (`Snapshot.eMechAntiLight`, `eMechDesignKnown`, `eMechSeenAgo`); `Plan` leans the Trooper share (armour vs anti-infantry Mech, rifles vs anti-armour); `MechAway` (seen within 15 s, >70 m from their base) opens a window wave at the Mech-less threshold, weighted to their base and bay; a wave stages on its own Mech when fit (`stageWithMech`). `Dbg.mechWindows/stagedWithMech` count them on the evaluation line.
- It does not start a wave at a Mech-held base while its own Mech is under 60%.
- Mech values stay out of `Snapshot.armyValue/eArmyValue` and the player-style read (`mechAlive`, `eMech`, `eMechBay` instead).
- Mech-brain dithering fix (the evaluation line gives the AI Mech's time by intent and by reason, `intentText` without numbers): withdrawing 30-49% at full hull was it dithering at the enemy Mech's reach, so a Mech that backs off keeps outside that reach for 12 s (`MechBrain.Shy`), goes in with its own attacking army (`withArmy`), and stays in a duel it started while the odds hold (`hold`). "Their Mech has the edge" = standing with its army because odds were under 0.8 even together while the army attacked on and the regroup call stayed silent (it fired only from 30 m): it now reads Holding once with the army, and `WarnOff` calls the regroup when its army is going at a Mech it cannot beat.
