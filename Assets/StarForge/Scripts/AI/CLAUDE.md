# AI (Commander, MechBrain) — area notes

Root `CLAUDE.md` holds the cross-cutting rules; this file holds the AI lessons. Mech rules/physics: `../World/Mech/CLAUDE.md`.

## Pipeline

`GameBootstrap.StartMatch` builds a `Commander` for team 1 (and one for team 0 when spectating), ticked from `GameWorld.Ticked`.
`Perception` (fog-limited memory) → `OpponentModel` (Bayesian filter, six player styles) → `StrategySelector` (contextual bandit, eight plans) → `Commander` (macro, scouting, tactics, micro).
`AIMemory` persists bandit weights and the player profile to `persistentDataPath/starforge_ai_memory.json` and seeds the next match's priors.

**Invariants for any Commander change:** enemy state only through `GameWorld.Visible`/`VisibleCell`, never `units`; every order through the public `GameWorld.Cmd*` the mouse uses, paid from `ActionBudget` (APM token bucket) and delayed by the per-difficulty reaction time.
**MechBrain is exempt by design:** omniscient, ~1,200 APM. The Commander learns about the Mech only through its advice.

**Variety.** `Commander.DrawPersonality` draws a `Personality` from the match seed (opening, unit-mix shifts, push timing, flank/second-prong taste, per-plan score offsets), avoiding the last two learned openings (`AIMemoryData.recentOpenings/recentPlans/recentWins`). `StrategySelector.SetBias` nudges away from recent plans; a plan two minutes without paying off grows stale. Waves (`StartWave/RunWave/EndWave`) pick target and approach from the influence map, stage, may split a prong. Memory off (benchmark, evaluation): only the seed varies it.

**AI internals are developer-only.** Anything exposing the AI's beliefs/plans, or letting a player disable/reset its memory, is gated on `MatchSettings.debugAI` (`-sfdebug` or the editor menu). Player-facing text: `HUDController.OpponentHelp` and the README "The opponent AI" section; keep both consistent with `Commander`.

**Statics persist across play sessions (domain reload off).** Trials silence the opponent with `Commander.Suspended`; one left on made a quick evaluation lose 3 of 4 to an AI that never moved. `AIEvalRunner`/`BenchmarkRunner` clear it; a one-off `Unity_RunCommand` trial must too. `Commander.IgnoreSites` is the same kind of A/B switch (runners reset it).

**Artillery that keeps missing is moved** (`Commander.RepositionStuck`). `Unit.shotsMissed` counts splash shells in a row, fired within 5 m of `missFrom`, that burst further than splash + radius from the target; it must not reset on `MoveTo` (units re-path every 1.2 s, so it never grew). After three, the tank gets a plain *move* (an attack-move halts at once when the target is in range) to the nearest spot round the target from which `GameWorld.ShellFallsShort` says the arc arrives (it follows Fire's arc; only 2 m under ground counts as blocked, since the real shell tests where it lands each frame and jumps thinner crests). `AgentPlay.BlockedShot` stages it and prints each burst; `ShellTrialOffSwitch` flips the A/B. Measured: off, 21 shells into the ridge in 45 s, none within 9 m; on, moved after three and killed. Match-level hit rates (`ShellTrial`, `Faction.shellHits/shellMisses`) diverge too much to show it.

## Mech AI

**The Mech takes no orders from its side**: every player `Cmd*` skips `def.Autonomous`. Its AI (`MechBrain.cs`, one per side, ticked by `GameBootstrap`) drives it only through `CmdMechMove`/`CmdMechFocus`/`CmdMechHalt` and speaks through `MechAdvise` (`GameEventKind.MechAdvice`): the HUD shows it as comms, `Commander.OnWorldEvent` weighs it (heed/ignore by `Personality.adviceTrust`).

**Two separate minds** (`MechDoctrine.cs`): team 0's (player's) is the guardian with MechBrain's original numbers; team 1's is a hunter, raider or brawler per match with jitter (repairs at ~30%, lower caution, readier duels, hunts from ~55-62% hull out to 170-210 m, Diggers/Foundries worth more, guard post 36-45 m out) — except while `homeThreatened`: it then neither hunts nor stands further out, so a bay raised in its base still pulls it home (BayEdgeCases case 0).
**Only the player's Mech talks.** Team 1's `Say` raises no event: its Attack/Defend/Regroup go straight to `Commander.HearMech` (`MechBrain.Listener`, set by `GameBootstrap`), same kind and place; it has no MechSpeech.

**Speech** (`MechSpeech.cs`): 406 morale lines tagged by situation and tone, chosen from a reading of the battle about once a second: standing 0.7 armies-and-Mechs / 0.3 bases (on all assets it read "even" through a match one side was losing); fights tracked by area, closed after 15 s with no death within 60 m (one map-wide feed never went quiet); turning points, extremis, near defeat, calm global or local to a guarding Mech; weights and cues. Own `Rng`; speaks through `MechBrain.Speak`, which must **not** push `nextAdvice` (speech must never delay the calls the Commander acts on). Kept rare: never within 30 s of any comms line, 90 s between its own, at most 4 in ten minutes.
Checks via `editor.sh call`: `MechSpeech.Check` (all there, filled in, every bucket reachable), `MechSpeech.JournalReport` (what the player's Mech said and why, calls, repeats, lines a minute).
Comms: a call about the same place within 150 s is `GameEvent.repeat` (a 1.6x bigger incoming force is news): HUD and radio drop it, a Commander hearing the event still gets it. The HUD tags calls (SUGGESTION/WARNING/REPORT) and shows flavour (Morale) quoted and muted (`comms-line--flair`), never pushing a call off the feed.

**Commander vs the enemy Mech.**
- While the enemy Mech is about, the Commander waits for a bigger army before pushing (`Plan`: x1.5 with its own Mech, x1.9 without, +20% per wave that Mech beat back, `mechRepulses`, up to four). Before the last factor it fed a defending Mech one wave at a time and matches ran to the cap.
- It plays against the Mech it has seen: `Perception` reads its guns (`Snapshot.eMechAntiLight`, `eMechDesignKnown`, `eMechSeenAgo`); `Plan` leans the Trooper share (armour vs anti-infantry Mech, rifles vs anti-armour); `MechAway` (seen within 15 s, >70 m from their base) opens a window wave at the Mech-less threshold, weighted to their base and bay; a wave stages on its own Mech when fit (`stageWithMech`). `Dbg.mechWindows/stagedWithMech` count them on the evaluation line.
- It does not start a wave at a Mech-held base while its own Mech is under 60%.
- Mech values stay out of `Snapshot.armyValue/eArmyValue` and the player-style read (`mechAlive`, `eMech`, `eMechBay` instead).
- Mech-brain dithering fix (the evaluation line gives the AI Mech's time by intent and by reason, `intentText` without numbers): withdrawing 30-49% at full hull was it dithering at the enemy Mech's reach, so a Mech that backs off keeps outside that reach for 12 s (`MechBrain.Shy`), goes in with its own attacking army (`withArmy`), and stays in a duel it started while the odds hold (`hold`). "Their Mech has the edge" = standing with its army because odds were under 0.8 even together while the army attacked on and the regroup call stayed silent (it fired only from 30 m): it now reads Holding once with the army, and `WarnOff` calls the regroup when its army is going at a Mech it cannot beat.

**A bay can go up anywhere a Digger can build, the enemy's base included.** `BayEdgeCases` (`Editor/MechTrials.cs`; `Only` picks a case) raises team 0's bay 12 m inside the enemy base, 45 m out, in a corner, on a lake shore, in a wood, far out on a flank (seed 1000, 420 s at 8x); reports bay and Mech fate, Mech time by reason, repair trips (a new docking after 10 s away), and for a Mech that fell its last intents. Cases 6 and 7 walk a Digger in at 90 s and place the bay on arrival, as a player would (a site goes up the moment it is ordered, so one ordered from afar is shot down first). Findings that stand:
- Home defence counted units only and ignored a bay in the base. It now counts an enemy Mech Bay (400) or Sentinel (250) within 55 m of its base, **only with an army worth `max(450, pushThreshold / 2)`** — without the gate it fed Troopers to the bay's tower one at a time and lost the match. MechBrain's `HomeThreat` counts an enemy bay (600) or tower (300) beside its structures.
- A site with no work done (`buildProgress <= 0`) does not use up the side's bay (`MechBookkeeping`), like one called off. `Remembered.unfinished` lets the Commander answer a site going up by its base with whatever army it has (`site`; the gate above is for finished guns): 45 m out, the Digger died 15-16 s after the order and the bay was never finished, against 19-39 s and finished once in three with `Commander.IgnoreSites`.
- The bay's gun (1,800 hp, 22 damage every 0.6 s, 24 m) kills a Digger in two shots, so `RunMacro` step 5b moves Diggers off ore an enemy gun covers (`guns`, `SafeOre`: node, its Foundry and the way between out of reach) and expansions avoid it; with no safe ore they stand clear — unless the gun covers the main Foundry, when they mine on at a loss (stood clear there, the army never grew and the base fell).
- A Mech mends only at a bay on its own half or one nothing out-guns it at (`BaySafe` counts everything whose reach covers the gantry, not a fixed 32 m: it sat in the gantry at 5-13% while their Mech shot it from 45 m and died there in 3 of 5 lake-shore runs). Losing hull for 6 s in a forward bay's gantry sends it away 20-120 s (`bayShunUntil`, doubling); hurt with no bay to go to it falls back to its own lines (`Guard(null)`); it guards its base, not a forward bay.
