# AI (Commander, MechBrain) — area notes

Root `CLAUDE.md` holds the cross-cutting rules; this file holds the AI lessons. Mech rules/physics: `../World/Mech/CLAUDE.md`.

## Pipeline

`GameBootstrap.StartMatch` builds a `Commander` for team 1 (and one for team 0 when spectating), ticked from `GameWorld.Ticked`.
`Perception` (fog-limited memory) → `OpponentModel` (Bayesian filter, six player styles) → `StrategySelector` (contextual bandit, eight plans) → `Commander` (macro, scouting, tactics, micro).
`AIMemory` persists bandit weights and the player profile to `persistentDataPath/starforge_ai_memory.json` and seeds the next match's priors.

**Invariants for any Commander change:** enemy state only through `GameWorld.Visible`/`VisibleCell`, never `units`; every order through the public `GameWorld.Cmd*` the mouse uses, paid from `ActionBudget` (APM token bucket) and delayed by the per-difficulty reaction time.
**MechBrain is exempt by design:** omniscient, ~1,200 APM. The Commander learns about the Mech only through its advice.
**Perception keeps one memory per sighting** (each takes the nearest entry of its type no other sighting took this pass, `claimed`): taking the first entry within 7 m folded a parked squad into one or two, so a raid read as a scout and home defence never diverted. **The influence map counts only finished armed enemy structures** (a Sentinel, a Mech Bay's gun; a site has no gun). Check `BugTrials.Sight`: every Trooper of a 3.4 m clump that is in sight remembered (9 of 9, 10 of 10), still so five passes later; threat at a bay site 0.00, the finished bay 3.99 (read through `ComputeCPU`: the GPU backend answers a frame or two late).

**Their Mech is believed about until it is seen to fall** (`Perception.Forget`: a Mech entry outlives the 50 s and seen-empty rules; it goes when a dying enemy Mech is in sight, `mechSeenFalling`, or after 300 s unseen), and **it has one entry** (a Mech sighting matches the Mech entry at any distance; the snapshot takes the freshest). Forgotten like a rifleman, the push threshold fell to the no-Mech one between waves and the next wave walked into a base the Mech still held; matched only within walking reach, a Mech seen across the map got a second, stale entry that stood in for it in the snapshot. `Perception.LegacyMechMemory` is the A/B (the evaluation runner resets it). Check the evaluation line's "their Mech alive ..., believed gone N% of it, waves started meanwhile M (K believing it gone)": five Macro games, 4-27% and 36 of 78 waves before, 0-2% and 0 of 47 after; Full 2026-10-21 0-10%, 2 of 141. Accepted: a Mech killed unseen holds the Mech threshold up to 300 s.

**Variety.** `Commander.DrawPersonality` draws a `Personality` from the match seed (opening, unit-mix shifts, push timing, flank/second-prong taste, per-plan score offsets), avoiding the last two learned openings (`AIMemoryData.recentOpenings/recentPlans/recentWins`). `StrategySelector.SetBias` nudges away from recent plans; a plan two minutes without paying off grows stale. Waves (`StartWave/RunWave/EndWave`) pick target and approach from the influence map, stage, may split a prong. Memory off (benchmark, evaluation): only the seed varies it.

**Side raids** (`RunTactics`, `sideRaid`): a personality with `raidTaste` > 0.5 (about half) builds two Skimmers after 150 s and from 180 s keeps exactly two raiding Diggers whatever the plan (non-Skimmers and extras go back to the army). The victim is the visible Digger nearest the raiders plus 25 m per unit of enemy influence on it (the one nearest their base's middle was the best guarded); driven off, the raid rests 25 s (`raidRestUntil`). A prong has its own `prongTarget` (sharing the raiders' re-ordered both squads every pass). Evaluation line: `raids N (M beside another plan)`. **A raid also pulls back when one of its raiders is under half hull and was hit in the last 2 s** (`bleeding`): by the field alone it never once pulled back in a trial and lost 7 Skimmers for 7 Diggers on one side, 3 for none on the other. ## Mech AI

**The Mech takes no orders from its side**: every player `Cmd*` skips `def.Autonomous`. Its AI (`MechBrain.cs`, one per side, ticked by `GameBootstrap`) drives it only through `CmdMechMove`/`CmdMechFocus`/`CmdMechHalt`. The player's Mech speaks through `MechAdvise` (`GameEventKind.MechAdvice`): the HUD shows it as comms, and a Commander on that side (spectating) hears it in `OnWorldEvent`. The opponent's Mech calls its Commander directly (below). Either way `Commander.HearMech` heeds or ignores by `Personality.adviceTrust`.

**Two minds, one judgement** (`MechDoctrine.cs`; the user's call): both Mechs fight the same cautious way -- more defending than attacking, going in when the moment calls for it -- but the opponent's is its own mind, not a copy, and not a different strategy. Team 0's keeps MechBrain's original numbers (`Guardian()`); team 1's draws a temper per match from the seed (`Tempered`: steady, wary or bold, then +-4% jitter, then **every number clamped to the band**: repair 0.34-0.42, odds x0.92-1.1, hunt from 0.68-0.78, reach 135-165 m, worth 135-170, guard 20-24 m, and their Mech never taken on at under 0.92 of the player's Mech's odds -- caution and the duel factor multiply, and unclamped a bold one went in at 0.83). An aggressive "hunter" doctrine was designed and rejected: do not make the opponent's Mech bolder than this band. Only repair (also where `BaySafe` sends a hurt Mech home), caution, duel, the hunt gate, hunt reach/worth and the guard post read the doctrine; the logic of `BaySafe`, `HomeSide`, the bay shun, `HomeThreat`, `thinkEvery` and `Punish` does not. `MechBrain.Debug` (replay trace, AI inspector) starts with the doctrine name; the per-match evaluation line does not print it.
