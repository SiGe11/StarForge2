# Benchmark, evaluation, adaptive resolution — area notes

Root `CLAUDE.md` has the commands and the short measuring checklist; this holds the reasoning and the baselines. Read it before interpreting any fps number or touching a quality preset.

## Measuring frame rate
- `-sfplay` keeps vsync and the 60 cap, as a match runs — the frame rate a player sees, and the one to compare builds on. Without it the run is uncapped with vsync off, which on 6000.6.1 comes out bimodal (~9/60 ms). Capped frames quantise: on the game's clock to 16.7, 25 and 33.3 ms on High (`frame lengths in whole ms`; "Adaptive resolution" below). Compare by the average and the render scale, against a build measured the same way; `-sfcap60` matches builds from before the uncapping fix. **Mechs land at ~9 minutes since the drop became seven minutes (2026-11-04; seed 1000 AI vs AI: bays 2:04 and 2:11, Mechs 9:07 and 9:15), so a 420 s run has no Mech in it; a run that should include them for as long as the old 420 s ones (about 3 minutes of Mech) needs ~740 s; the gallery's `9_*` Mech shots wait for the landing (`BenchmarkRunner`: its Mech wait is the old 300 s plus `MechDropDelay`) and are unrun since the change.** Every baseline below was a 420 s run with the Mechs down at ~4 minutes (the old two-minute drop): do not compare them with a run of another length or without a Mech, and keep a 420 s pair for what has no Mech in it.
- **Record `pmset -g batt` with every number**: runs while the battery charged came out ~3 fps lower; on battery the Mac turns Low Power Mode on, and a long evaluation drains it even on the charger.
- **A locked screen caps the player at 20 fps** (every frame 50 ms; two runs came out at exactly 20.0 when the keep-awake ended and the Mac locked). Check `ioreg -n Root -d1 -a | plutil -extract IOConsoleUsers json -o - -` for `CGSSessionScreenIsLocked` before believing a slow run; unlocking needs the user.
- The Neo is fanless and drifts ~1 ms as it heats: compare rendering changes in alternating pairs against the previous build.
- **A player that is not the front app measures nothing.** Started from a background process it stays behind whatever is in front, and behind the user's Safari its window is throttled: flat 16.67 ms ("60 fps") at render scale 1.00, about 30 frames a second counted, one frame up to 46 s. Run `open -a <app>` a few seconds after launch and count the samples it was not in front (`not_front`, 2026-11-04: 2 of 4 runs void, the rest partly). A gallery behind another app still took its pictures, with different timing. **A multi-second "frame" after time behind another app is likely the clock catching up, not a freeze:** one run with Claude in front for 10-30 s logged a 7,253 ms frame, yet its worst 5 s read 32 fps, about the rate counted behind another app, where a real 7 s stop would read 0. Check `not_front` before hunting a stall.
- The report's "simulation ..." and "frames over 40 ms ..." lines (`GameWorld.SimMs/TickedMs`, `GameBootstrap.MechBrainMs`, GCs, NavMesh island builds) say whether a long frame had a slow simulation step. The `frame timing:` line (`FrameTimingManager`; `BuildMac` sets `enableFrameTimingStats`) gives main/render thread and present-wait; **its GPU time is not usable on Metal** (frames overlap: GPU times exceed the frame).
- Mech fights are bound by cost that does not shrink with resolution; measure shadow distance and grass with `-sfshadows N` / `-sfdensity X`.
- Flicker/shimmer: measure on the built player (command in root file). Editor captures did not reproduce shimmer the player showed. The burst holds the camera wherever the fighting had it; `-sfburstbase` holds it on the player's base as the gallery's `1_base_close` frames it (the Diggers' tread marks, ore, the Foundry): there a Digger driving through the frame and the buildings' animated lights make 10-12% 'vibrating' on their own, so read the heatmap over the thing being judged, not the total.
- Gallery: use the player's `-sfgallery`, not editor captures (the Editor's Game view is ~810x375). Takes the same shots every run on the default map (base close/mid, ore, shore, a grove before/during/after fire, animal, songbird flock feeding then flushed, overview, first fire-fight, the Mechs `9_mech_close`/`9b_mech_mid`/`9c_mech_other`/`9d_mech_fight_*`/`9e_mech_bay`).
- **Test runs are muted** (`TestAudio`, before the first scene): `-sfbench`, `-sfgallery(quick)`, `-sfburst`, `-sfshot`, `-sfscreentest` set `AudioListener.volume` 0 unless `-sfsound`; `-sfmute` mutes any run. Only the output goes: every source, voice and the mixer still run (`RifleCheck` in the Editor at listener volume 0 against 1: 0 virtualised either way, 16 and 15 real voices at the busiest), so frame times should compare with earlier runs; no muted/unmuted fps pair has been run (a `-sfsound` run plays aloud). A 20 s `-sfbench -sfplay` muted: 979 frames, 0 exceptions. Nothing else in the game sets the listener volume (AudioDirector scales each source by `masterVolume`).
- `-sfscreentest` (no `-sfbench`) switches full screen and window four times, logs the sizes the player reports and quits; pass `-logFile` to read them (the terminal has no screen-recording permission, so that log is the check).
- Testing cheat (README, `World/GameWorld.Cheat.cs`): `Ctrl`/`Cmd`+`Shift`+`M`, +5000 ore, +100 supply, 5x training on and off, marks `GameWorld.cheated`, and a win with it becomes the reckoning. **For a test that needs ore and a normal ending, use `CheatOre`** (no reckoning, no fast training). In play every match draws a new seed (the AI's per-match personality); benchmark and evaluation use `MatchSettings.fixedSeed`.

## Evaluation runner (`AIEvalRunner`)
**A run stopped part-way must not take over the next Play** (statics survive play sessions): `active` is cleared on every entry into play mode (`ClearOnPlay`, `InitializeOnEnterPlayMode`), a wanted run coming in through the prefs `AIEvalMenu.Run` sets, and the player's own `MatchSettings` (memory, fixed seed and seed, difficulty) are saved when a run starts and put back when it ends or is cut short. Before, a plain Play after a stopped run came up Playing at 8x with its side scripted, memory off and the seed fixed. Check `BugTrials.EvalAbortState` around a Quick run stopped after 20 s: then a plain Play reads Menu, 1x, nothing pending, the settings as before the run; a Quick run to the end leaves them as before too (2/4 won, 0 lost, 0 exceptions).


## Adaptive resolution (`View/AdaptiveResolution.cs`)
Every preset aims at one 60 Hz refresh (`targetMs` 16.9). Under vsync a frame shows for 16.7 or 33.3 ms, so load is judged by the share of frames that miss a refresh (`SyncedUpdate`: down past `missHigh`, up after a clean spell under `missLow`; High 0.60/0.35, the others 0.15/0.04; at 60 Hz a miss share m is 60/(1+m) fps, so 60% is 37.5 and 71% is 35). The old length rule could never see room to climb (a vsynced frame never measures under 16.7 ms) and one heavy fight left a match at its floor. *High* once aimed at 21.5 ms ("~46 fps", 160 m shadows, floor 0.80): 47.6 uncapped but 39 in play.
**`missHigh` is a trigger, not the level the share settles at, and `missLow` decides whether a fight can climb.** The share settles well under `missHigh` (17% at 0.28, 44% at 0.60, 53% at 0.70), since one step down frees a lot. The scale climbs only under `missLow`, so that has to sit above a fight's share at its present scale: at 0.10 a Mech fight stays at 0.55-0.70. Past `missHigh` 0.70 a fight could sit under 35 fps with the resolution unmoved.
**The aim is the user's choice: High 0.60/0.35 since 2026-11-04, picked from the numbers below once 35 fps was enough (it was 0.28/0.10).** The margin above 35 goes to resolution, so **about 42 fps on High is the design, not a regression from 51**; a new cost or a saving shows mostly in the scale until it reaches 0.55, so compare builds by both. When changing it, keep the worst 5 s at 35 or above. Try an aim with `-sfmisshigh`/`-sfmisslow`; the report prints the thresholds, scale changes, time at each scale, frames by refreshes and the frame-rate floor (worst 5 s and 30 s). `-sfbench 420 -sfplay`, seed 1000, *High*, 6000.6.8f1, merged models, one frozen build, AC 100% charged, mirror order, player in front, 0 voided, 2026-11-04, two runs each:

| `missHigh`/`missLow` | fps | Mech in view | scale (Mech in view) | frames over 40 ms | worst 5 s | worst 30 s | frames 25 ms or less |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.28/0.10 | 51.4, 51.2 | 51.7, 50.6 | 0.63, 0.64 (0.59, 0.61) | 40, 28 | 46.2, 45.2 | 47.3, 47.6 | 83% |
| 0.60/0.35 | 41.5, 41.8 | 41.3, 42.2 | 0.76, 0.77 (0.72, 0.73) | 39, 39 | 36.4, 36.8 | 39.0, 38.5 | 56% |
| 0.70/0.45 | 38.8, 39.4 | 40.2, 38.9 | 0.83, 0.80 (0.77, 0.77) | 36, 48 | 34.6, 34.2 | 36.1, 36.1 | 45-48% |

So against the old aim, 1.45x the pixels at 0.60 and 1.65x at 0.70, in a Mech fight as in the match's average; 0.70/0.45 was left out for falling under 35 in the worst 5 s. The preset with no aim flags, on its own builds (AC charged, 0 exceptions): seed 1000 42.0 fps, 41.8 with a Mech in view, scale 0.78 (0.74), worst 5 s 36.6, 30 s 38.5, 40 over 40 ms; **a 20-minute match on seed 1074** 41.9, 41.6, 0.78 (0.73), 37.0, 38.5, 27, lowest scale 0.70, the next climb still wanting 4 s at the end, so no stranding; **rain on seed 1000** 41.7, 41.9, 0.77 (0.72), 36.6, 37.6, 36. Frames over 40 ms do not follow the aim: roughly 40-80% of them fall on a frame with a GC, and as many with a NavMesh island build. With 200 rigged soldiers following the fight (`-sfrigprobe 200 -sfrigfollow`, one run each, 0.70 / 0.60 / old, not alternated): 0.70 38.9 fps (38.5 Mech in view), scale 0.71 (0.69), worst 5 s 34.4, 30 s 36.6, 59 over 40 ms; 0.60 41.4 (41.9), 0.67 (0.62), 37.0, 37.5, 52; old 49.0 (46.5), 0.56 (0.55), 41.6, 42.8, 54. **The floor follows the aim until the scale runs out:** at 0.60 and 0.70 the soldiers came off the resolution, lowest 0.60, so 0.05 of it is left; the old aim spent all of it, 100% of Mech-in-view time at 0.55. Per pixel the two steps cost about the same (51 to 42 fps for 1.45x, 42 to 39 for 1.14x more); what stops the aim at 0.60 is the worst 5 s.
**The share is frames over 25 ms on the game's clock, not frames on screen for two refreshes.** At 0.60/0.35 two runs read 15-17 ms 32%, 24-25 ms 49%, 32-33 ms 18%. So a third mode sits on `SyncedUpdate`'s test (`ms > refresh * 1.5`, 25 ms at 60 Hz) and the test cuts it in two, 24.1-24.6% each side. The share still ordered the aims and read within about two points across seeds, rain and a 20-minute match (43-44.5%), but **anything moving that mode by a fraction of a millisecond could move the share by many points, against a band of 25 between `missLow` and `missHigh`: read this line again after an engine upgrade or a pacing change.** Why a mode at 25 ms exists on a panel the OS reports at 60 Hz (at 120 every frame would count as missed), and what the panel shows for such a frame, is unmeasured. No run behind other apps, of Balanced or Battery at this build, or of `-sftactical` at this aim.
**Sharpness:** `-sfholdscale S` turns the controller off and holds S (a gallery's `[Gallery]` log lines give each shot's scale). Mean absolute Laplacian of luma over the frame's middle, against 0.60: 0.72 +13-19%, 0.77 +17-27%, 1.00 +74-99%, on the shots that frame the same ground every run (base close and mid, shore, burnt grove, overview: three gallery runs at 0.65 agree within 6%). Fight and Mech shots frame what the match is doing then (0.55-1.2 at one scale): compare those by eye, not frame by frame.
