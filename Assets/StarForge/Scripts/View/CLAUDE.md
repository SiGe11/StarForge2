# View (FX, audio, camera, rendering helpers) — area notes

Shader-side rules: `../../Shaders/CLAUDE.md`. Wind/fire/felling simulation: `../World/CLAUDE.md`. Building the sound files: `../../../../Tools/CLAUDE.md`. Quality presets / adaptive resolution / benchmark numbers: `../Game/CLAUDE.md`.
Editor effect captures: `Camera.Render` into a RenderTexture from `Unity_RunCommand` misses particles and overlays drawn in `LateUpdate`. Use `ScreenCapture.CaptureScreenshot` with a low `Time.timeScale`, and check `EditorApplication.isPlaying` first (`GameBootstrap.StartMatch` in edit mode spawns units into the open scene). A new shader compiles asynchronously (instanced trees drew only shadows for a few seconds): wait before judging a capture.
**Look-helpers never restart a running match** (`AgentPlay.*Look/Show/Check`): units left over from a restarted match throw from `UnitView.LateUpdate` every frame for the rest of the session. After writing a script, wait for `compiling=False` before `play`, or the Editor enters play on the old assembly without your helper.

## Blast pressure (`SF_Wind.hlsl`, shared by SF_Tree and SF_Grass)
A muzzle blast or burst queues a front in `FXDirector.PressureWave` (expands at 52 m/s, spends itself as it spreads). `UploadGusts` puts the four strongest into `_SF_Gusts` (xy origin, z front radius now, w shove in metres) and `_SF_GustShape` (xy heading, z how much it favours it, w thickness); `_SF_GustCount` is 0 the rest of the time so the loop costs a compare. A plant reads fronts from its own root position (no per-plant state; a seed replays identically). The shove goes with the square of a blade's height and a tree vertex's distance from the trunk's foot (roots stay planted); both shaders renormalise the displaced vertex to its old distance from the root (a hard push bends, not stretches). **Clear the globals when the match stops**, or the field stays bent on the end screen.
**The pressure draws nothing of its own** (no drawn ring — `SF_GroundDecal` kind 5 is retired — and no screen-space refraction in the fog pass). It *moves things* as it arrives: `FXDirector.PressureSweep` follows each front's annulus (`Gust.swept`) and ruffles water (small dark rings — a roughened surface reflects less sky — a few faint bright ones and spray; the wake's white rings made the lake look like snow), lifts dust off dry ground with clods close in, tears leaves from crowns it crosses, jolts the camera when it reaches `RTSCamera.Focus`. Anything new it should move goes there; anything that would only *show* the front does not. `AgentPlay.PressureLook` holds a front still at several strengths with FXDirector off, each against the next frame with it off — the only way to see what the shove alone did (`Temp/pressure_*.png`).

## Smoke is the thing most easily got wrong
- `FX_Smoke` runs at `_Density` 1.4, `_SoftFade` 1.0 (set in `SceneAssembler`): the atlas feathers so wide that at 1.0 every plume was too faint on sunlit ground.
- The soft fade blends a puff out where geometry sits just behind it, so **smoke born on geometry is invisible** (the Mauler's exhaust, born on its engine deck, was unseen until moved 0.5 m above the stacks). `AgentPlay.MaulerShowReport` counts particles above the deck. Measure before re-tuning something you cannot see.
- Atlas `Art/Textures/smoke_puffs.png` (normal, occlusion, coverage; `python3 Tools/make_smoke_puffs.py`): coverage **never reaches opaque**, outline eroded at two scales; `SF_Smoke` must not rescale coverage back to 1 (it once divided by 0.6) or a puff draws as a solid sprite and a burst as a hard dark lump. Billows are warped ellipses over a domain-warped field, not hemispheres (the first version's were countable; its value noise showed a star in every puff).
- Lit by sun and from above, picked and eroded per particle through custom vertex streams (`FXDirector.SmokeStreams`: stable random, age). With the sun behind the camera every billow faces it and the cloud goes flat; the light from above keeps the volume. Particle colours are grey 0.3-0.5, not near-black (a 0.17 grey came out as a hole in the scene). Fire and glows still use photographed sheets (`SF_Particle`) / flipbooks (`SF_Flipbook`).
- When cutting smoke back, cut what comes off moving units (exhaust, track dust, shell trails), **not** what an explosion or fire makes: that is where the drama lives.

## Unit deaths (`UnitWreck`)
On the frame a mobile unit dies `UnitView` hands its `body` (and the Mauler's turret / the Trooper's `Gun`, its legs) to a new wreck object and does nothing more; `GameWorld` still destroys the unit after 1.3 s and the simulation is unchanged. The wreck is rigid bodies on the terrain collider (follows craters): a box from the mesh bounds (readable when mesh data is not), centre of mass low, raised out of the ground if the NavMesh had it buried, frozen kinematic once still (or at 7 s) and sunk. It falls the way `Unit.killDir` points, as hard as `killForce` (set by `GameWorld.Kill`: away from a shell's burst when `Damage(..., splash)`, else from the shooter; fallback direction from the unit id, never the match's random stream). A Trooper's topple is given about his feet (linear velocity `fall x spin x hips`): spun about his middle his feet dug in and he stopped at 25 degrees. Machines char through `_Damage` (0.9 falling to 0.6 so glowing cracks go out) with a low `_Burn` (0.32 to 0): at the debris' full `_Burn` a hull glowed like lava and bloomed to a yellow lump. `FXDirector.Wrecks` burns/smokes by `Heat` and dusts a skidding Skimmer; `TrooperDown` replaces the fireball a rifleman used to die in (AudioDirector and GroundMask skip the boom and scorch for him). Only what the player saw die leaves a wreck; past 36 the oldest sink early. `AgentPlay.DeathLook` photographs one of each (`Temp/death_*.png`, 2x).

## Mauler weight
`FXDirector.MaulerEffects` (engine smoke off the two stacks, dust and stones off the tracks, barrel smoke after a shot) + the hull's recoil rock in `UnitView` (`RecoilRock`, damped swing; the turret takes its yaw as a *local* rotation so it rides the hull). Stack and track positions are the model's (`Tools/blender/build_models.py`: stacks 0.62 m out on the engine deck, track runs 1.30 m out): moving them in Blender means moving them here. Engine smoke goes through the `trail` system, not `smoke` (a base on fire fills `smoke` itself, and a burning structure is the cue the player needs at a distance); the rate thins with camera distance (`RTSCamera.distance`). Tuning landed on the third round (cloud the size of the tank → unseen haze → a wisp at idle and a grey-black plume under load). The ground keeps the trail: tread marks last 60 s, `GroundMask` stamps channel A (a crater's thrown soil) along a Mauler's path, healing over minutes. Sound: `AudioDirector.TankVoice` — the three loudest Maulers in earshot get an engine and a track source each, engine pitch/level following load, clatter following speed (`AgentPlay.TankVoices`). `AgentPlay.MaulerShow`/`MaulerShowReport` stage one in a grove (idling, driving, firing, shell landing; `Temp/mauler_*.png`); `AgentPlay.SmokeLook` photographs a shell burst, a structure going up, a tree burning (`Temp/smoke_*.png`).

## Fauna (`Fauna.cs`)
Quaternius FBX in `Art/Fauna/`, imported Legacy-animated with no materials; `SceneAssembler.BuildFauna` colours the parts, turns each model to face +Z (rig bones are unnamed, so each facing is given), sizes it from its skeleton (imported renderer bounds are metres off), saves `Prefabs/Fauna_*`. Ground animals step only on NavMesh for ground units; flyers keep level over the highest ground under their circle. Songbirds (`perches`) feed on the ground (hop, peck, look), flush as a flock, fly a bounding flight to another open patch and land: they need `fold`/`peck` clips and open spots (`Vegetation.UnderCrown`). Animals are drawn about twice life size or they vanish into the grass at this camera. View-only and fog-fair: shown and frightened only by what the player could see. `-sfnofauna` omits them (~0.3 ms).

## Audio (`AudioDirector`)
- `AudioDirector.bank` (a `SoundBank` SceneAssembler fills from `Audio/`) with synth fallbacks. `PlayAt` takes a free voice or the one nearest its end (`FreeVoice`), never simply the next in turn; idle tank loops are paused.
- **Loudness is relative**: "the rifle has no sound" was a shot at 0.15 under music at 0.54, not a missing clip. `AgentPlay.RifleCheck`/`RifleCheckReport` stage a fire-fight and report every shot's source (started, audible, virtualised) before anything is changed. `AgentPlay.AudioReport` prints what the bank actually loaded (the only check that `make_audio.py`'s output is wired in).
- Music: `music_<calm|tension|combat>_<n>` tracks, one mood at a time on two crossfading sources, each at `musicLoudness` / its RMS from `music.json`; the benchmark report prints how much each mood played. The calm set (three, played in turn) must be warm: a minor-key piano loop under a quiet base reads as creepy (see `Tools/CLAUDE.md` for the measuring tool and thresholds).

## Colour and glow
- Glow is emission read by bloom (threshold 1, clamped at 24). Ore uses `SF_Unit`'s `_Crystal` path, reading per-shard data from vertex colour G/B (`sf_model.set_vdata`); the Digger's `ore_glow` material follows `_OreGlow`, set per renderer by `UnitView`. The crystal *emission*, not the albedo, carries the colour (glossy facets reflecting the pale sky washed it to white).
- **Ore colour lives in two places that must agree:** `SFMaterialLibrary` (crystal and ore_glow emission, violet rim) and `FXDirector.OreLight/OreRim`; the ground pool's breath (`FXDirector.OreBreath`) uses the crystal shader's phase and period.
- WASD pans the camera (unless a modifier is held): command hotkeys must not use W, A, S or D.
