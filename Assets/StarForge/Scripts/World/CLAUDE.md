# World (rules, navigation, vegetation, fire) — area notes

Map generation: `MapGen/CLAUDE.md`. Mechs: `Mech/CLAUDE.md`. Shader/draw side of wind, blasts, trees: `../View/CLAUDE.md`, `../../Shaders/CLAUDE.md`.

## Simulation
`GameWorld` (`[DefaultExecutionOrder(-50)]`) owns all match state and ticks every `Unit` itself in `Update`, `dt` clamped to 0.1 s: cache positions → rebuild spatial hash → `Unit.Tick` → compact dead units, rebuild hash → projectiles → visibility (every 0.12 s) → supply → win check → `Ticked`. `Unit` = order logic on a `NavMeshAgent`; `UnitView` = presentation only and **must never write to the `Unit`**. Gameplay effects leave the world only as `GameWorld.Event` (`GameEvent`), consumed by `FXDirector`, `AudioDirector`, `GroundMask`, `HUDController`.
Anything needing its own randomness uses its own stream seeded from the match seed (`fireRng`, wreck directions from the unit id), never the match's stream, or a seeded match shifts.

## Navigation areas
- Boulders (`Boulder.cs`) and trunks of trees on walkable ground are left out of the bake and stand on the `Rubble` area (`NavMeshModifierVolume`s; trees' all sit on `Map/Vegetation`); every agent except the Mauler excludes it. `GameWorld` crushes a rock and `Vegetation` fells a tree when a Mauler reaches it or a blast does, then `GameWorld.RequestNavRebuild` rebuilds the tiles (`NavMeshSurface.UpdateNavMesh`, at most every 1.5 s: `navRebuildNotBefore`). `GameWorld.Awake` swaps in a copy of the baked data first, so a match never edits the asset; it also sets `NavMesh.pathfindingIterationsPerFrame` to 1000 (Unity's 100 let a rebuild restart long requests: Maulers in a wood waited up to 9 s).
- **A rebuild takes the path from every agent whose way crossed a changed tile** (`PathInvalid`, no path, nothing pending), and a Mauler fells its own trees: **never read "no path" as arrival.** `Unit.KeepGoing` re-requests it and ends a move order only at the goal, at the end of a partial path, or after four empty answers (before it, orders dropped mid-journey and units stood until clicked again).
- `AgentPlay.OrderTrial` sends a player army through woods and rocks for three minutes and reports order latency, stands and drops; `AgentPlay.StallTrial` does it for AI-vs-AI (counts path requests by unit and order).
- Water is not baked: the lake bed is ground (units wade up to `MapBuilder.WadeDepth`); deeper water is Not Walkable volumes on `Map/DeepWater`. Placement and walkability queries use `GameWorld.GroundAreas`; structures need dry ground.

## Vegetation and craters
`Vegetation` holds the plants MapBuilder placed (serialized `Plant` structs, per-kind meshes/colours) and their runtime state: felling, fire, burning out. Ticked by `GameWorld`, which also owns `Blast(...)` (what an explosion does to rocks, plants, ground; called from splash hits and `Kill`). Drawing: `View/VegetationRenderer` (GPU-instanced per kind, `SF_Tree`, remembers each plant as the player last saw it). `GroundDeformer` (added by `GameWorld.Awake`) digs craters into a runtime copy of the TerrainData; units ride the unchanged NavMesh, so `UnitView` sinks models by `Drop()`, and ground clutter, boulders, plants move by `LastChange()` on `Deformed`. Craters lower the ground only to the deepest single bowl over it (`GroundDeformer.Crater`); the blasted look is `GroundMask` channel A in `SF_Terrain`.
`Vegetation` state that needs the map (grass fuel, wetness) is built in `EnsureGrass` on the first tick, **not `Awake`** (`MapInfo.Instance` may not be set yet).
Trees close `PlantKind.blockRadius` (trunk + low boughs + a unit's radius) as Rubble; groves that would disconnect a base, expansion or ore field are dropped (`MapGenerator.Blockage`). MapChecks tests a ring 0.4 m outside each trunk and base-to-base connectivity. Undergrowth kinds set `drawDistance` and `castShadows = false`. **Plant kind indices are MapGenerator's constants: append new kinds, never reorder.**

## Felling
- **Blasts fell trees by chance, not radius** (`Vegetation.Blast`): the roll falls off with distance over `radius * 1.35` and with trunk thickness; odds scale with `0.38 / trunkRadius`, so a kind's `trunkRadius` is gameplay as well as the blocking ring. `AgentPlay.FellTrial` shells a wood 60 times and prints the rate per distance band: aim for ~80/35/17/8% out to 4.3 m for a Mauler's shell (with scanned trunks, broadleaf 0.48 m / jacaranda 0.40: 66/45/21/6%, 27% overall on a generated map).
- **A felled tree** (`Vegetation.Topple`) is a rod pivoting on its stump: angular acceleration `1.5 g sin(theta) / L` (slow start, faster later, tall takes longer; 1.5-1.7 s when a blast throws it). `Fell(..., push)` = shove in rad/s (blast 0.5-1.3 by distance; a burned-through snag 0.05, then downwind). It rests on its boughs (`Live.rest`), bounces once or twice; the rebound is capped at 0.45 rad/s (a tree flung down at 6 rad/s sprang back 45 degrees and fell again). `LodgeCheck` hangs it up in a neighbour's crown if one stands in the way (`Live.lodged`, `slipAt`) until it slips or that tree goes. `GameEvent.speed` carries the crown's impact speed, scaling dust, debris and camera shake.
- **A Mauler shoulders a tree over** (`Vegetation.CrushUnderMaulers`), kinematic contact not a shove: when the trunk meets the hull's nose box (`HullFront` 2.2, `HullSide` 1.6 m, from the model's track runs) it is `Fell` with no push and `Live.pushedBy` the tank; while held its lean is at least `atan(past / GlacisHeight)` (`past` = how far the nose is beyond the trunk's foot), so the trunk never enters the hull; gravity then drops it ahead. The blow multiplies `agent.velocity` by 0.3-0.7 by trunk radius; `Unit.pushLoad` (reset per tick, the roots' resistance) cuts `Unit.UpdateFacing`'s pace by up to 75%, works the engine (`FXDirector.MaulerEffects`) and lifts the nose (`UnitView`). Slowing only top speed let the tank lose nothing before the tree was over. A trunk under the footprint is pressed in (`Live.crush`, sinking up to 0.9 trunk radii) and raises `GameEventKind.PlantCrushed` (splinters, `bank.crush`). `AgentPlay.PushLook` drives one into a lone blocking tree and prints lean vs hull position, speed and the closest the trunk came to the glacis (measured: 4.1 m/s down to 1.4-2.0, down 1.4-1.8 s after contact, gap never below 0.27 m; `Temp/push_*.png`).

## Wind (`Wind.cs`)
One heading, strength and gust rhythm for the whole match, drawn from the map seed in `MapGenerator.Generate`, evaluated as a pure function of the match clock (no state; a seed replays identically). Everything that should agree reads it: fire spreads downwind and faster in a gust, smoke/embers lean with it, a falling tree leans with it, leaves tear off crowns when it blows hard (`FXDirector.WindBlown`), cloud shadows drift with it, `Atmosphere` uploads `_SF_Wind` (xy heading, z strength, w gust phase) for SF_Tree/SF_Grass (both fall back to a steady breeze when it is zero, so nothing stands frozen in the scene view). Sway = per-material `_WindStrength` x that strength. **Clouds are the exception**: they drift steadily with `Wind.Weather` (the day's strength) at `Atmosphere.cloudDrift` (3.2 m/s on the windiest day); driven by `Wind.Speed` at 14 m/s they surged and stalled with every gust ("wind is too fast"). Gust phase advances at `0.45 + 0.95 x speed` (`0.5 + 1.4 x` thrashed on a windy day).

## Fire (`Vegetation.Ignite/Spread/TickGrass`)
- Every kind burns at `PlantKind.burns`; spread runs downwind (`Vegetation.Wind`); each blaze draws a `vigour` its spread inherits, so most fires die in a couple of plants and a few run; vigorous ones throw embers downwind across gaps. Grass carries fire between stands: a fuel grid (`GrassCell` 3 m) built on the first tick, burning cell to cell and lighting plants it reaches.
- **Fire burns only where grass grows.** Fuel comes from `MapGenerator.GrassChance` (the rule GroundScatter places tufts by: the terrain's splat weights with the same water/slope/boulder cut-outs, looked at in nine 1 m squares a cell, `grassMask`). A cell burns only if `MinExpectedTufts` (2.5) tufts are expected at full density; flames stand only on its grown squares (`GrassTuft`); SF_Terrain lays ash only on the lichen layer. (The old fuel — meadow weight + a quarter of gravel — burned 4,051 cells, 1,661 bare; now 1,783, 4 bare. `AgentPlay.GrassFuelCheck` counts tufts per burnable cell and sweeps the threshold.) Drawn tufts are random and thinner on Balanced/Battery, so the match cannot be tuft-for-tuft; **the simulation must not depend on the preset.** Grass under structures and ore seams is cleared from fuel (`ClearGrassUnder`, from `GameWorld.Spawn` and `EnsureGrass`).
- **Grid fields are `[NonSerialized]`:** entering play mode the editor round-trips private fields, turning a null array into an empty one that looked built and indexed -1.
- Damp resists: `Wetness` (water level against ground at the spot and 5 m round it) cuts a cell's fuel and a plant's catch chance, so shores and reed beds barely take.
- Limits are per fire: each blaze takes at most `blazeCapPlants`/`blazeCapCells` (a sixth of the map's growth), so one fire cannot burn everything but a match's fires together can; `MaxBurning` (45) plants and `MaxGrassFires` (150) cells at once. Burnt grass never comes back: `GroundMask` uploads the grid as `_SF_BurntGrass` (SF_Grass: stubble and char; SF_Terrain: ash), which does not heal like the mask's burn.
- `AgentPlay.FireTrial`/`FireTrialReport` light 30 fires at 8x and report plants and grass cells each: aim for a median of a few plants, a long tail, most of the map gone after 30.
- **Structures catch from wildfire** (`GameWorld.StructureFires`, `Unit.burnUntil`): while a burning plant's crown or burning grass is within `FireReach` (1.5 m) of a footprint, every `FireCheck` (0.5 s) rolls `1 - exp(-CatchRate * heat * 0.5)` (`Vegetation.FireNear` gives heat). A fire reaching a wall delivers 7-24 heat-seconds, so at `CatchRate` 0.025 about 29% set it alight; **judge a change by that expected share (`AgentPlay.BuildingFireTrial` prints it), not the caught-count, which over 8-16 fires is noise.** Alight: burns 13-19 s for 6-12% of health, never below 1 hp (fire alone does not destroy a building), then safe for 45 s. `StructureIgnited` drives the alert, flare, `FXDirector.BuildingFire` and the fire bed in `AudioDirector`. The flame sprite's base is the bottom of its quad, so tongues stand on the top of the middle and low on the outer walls (stepped rounds: at full height over the lower ring they hung in the air; the building hides tongues inside its footprint; meshes are not readable in the player, so the roof cannot be sampled). `AgentPlay.BuildingFireLook` photographs (`Temp/building_burn_*.png`).
- Burning plants draw tongues from `flames.png` (`FXDirector.MakeFlames`, vertical billboards, `startSize3D` for tall quads; the flame fills about half its quad). Smouldering after a fire goes out is tracked in FXDirector, not the simulation.
