# World (rules, navigation, vegetation, fire) — area notes

Map generation: `MapGen/CLAUDE.md`. Mechs: `Mech/CLAUDE.md`. Shader/draw side of wind, blasts, trees: `../View/CLAUDE.md`, `../../Shaders/CLAUDE.md`.

## Simulation
`GameWorld` (`[DefaultExecutionOrder(-50)]`) owns all match state and ticks every `Unit` itself in `Update`, `dt` clamped to 0.1 s: cache positions → rebuild spatial hash → `Unit.Tick` → compact dead units, rebuild hash → projectiles → visibility (every 0.12 s) → supply → win check → `Ticked`. `Unit` = order logic on a `NavMeshAgent`; `UnitView` = presentation only and **must never write to the `Unit`**. Gameplay effects leave the world only as `GameWorld.Event` (`GameEvent`), consumed by `FXDirector`, `AudioDirector`, `GroundMask`, `HUDController`.
Anything needing its own randomness uses its own stream seeded from the match seed (`fireRng`, wreck directions from the unit id), never the match's stream, or a seeded match shifts.
**Kept state that must be told when its source changes** (else it goes stale with no error): anything writing the visibility grid (`vis`/`explored`; only `UpdateVisibility` does) bumps `GameWorld.VisVersion`, which `FogOfWarRenderer` skips frames on; every path into `units` (`BeginMatch`, `Spawn`) adds a Foundry to `foundries`, which `NearestDropoff` reads; a map generated in place mid-play goes through `Vegetation.PlantsReplaced` (`MapGenerator.Generate` calls it; before it, trials that regenerated indexed past `live` every frame). Check: `MathChecks.Run`/`Report` (Editor, play mode, ~50 s), 0 mismatches against the full maths and 0 exceptions.

## Navigation areas
- Boulders (`Boulder.cs`) and trunks of trees on walkable ground are left out of the bake and stand on the `Rubble` area (`NavMeshModifierVolume`s; trees' all sit on `Map/Vegetation`); every agent except the Mauler excludes it. `GameWorld` crushes a rock and `Vegetation` fells a tree when a Mauler reaches it or a blast does, then `GameWorld.RequestNavRebuild` rebuilds the tiles (`NavMeshSurface.UpdateNavMesh`, at most every 1.5 s: `navRebuildNotBefore`). `GameWorld.Awake` swaps in a copy of the baked data first, so a match never edits the asset; it also sets `NavMesh.pathfindingIterationsPerFrame` to 1000 (Unity's 100 let a rebuild restart long requests: Maulers in a wood waited up to 9 s).
- **The ore model is imported readable** (`SFAssetPostprocessor`; every other model is not): a runtime NavMesh rebuild now and then took an ore node's meshes in spite of its `ignoreFromBuild` modifier ("Source mesh ORE / Crystals does not allow read access", about 6 in ~60 evaluation games; the cause was never caught -- `BugTrials.NavSources` found every ore mesh covered at all 83 polls of a Quick evaluation), and the Editor reads such a mesh while a player cannot, so the two built different tiles. Readable, both build the same, and the ore's carving obstacle cuts the same hole either way. Check `BugTrials.OreSourceRead` (one ore node's modifier switched off, a rebuild asked for): 2 read-access errors before, 0 now. `ReimportOreModel` reimports it under the rule.
- **A rebuild takes the path from every agent whose way crossed a changed tile** (`PathInvalid`, no path, nothing pending), and a Mauler fells its own trees: **never read "no path" as arrival.** `Unit.KeepGoing` re-requests it and ends a move order only at the goal, at the end of a partial path, or after four empty answers (before it, orders dropped mid-journey and units stood until clicked again).
- `AgentPlay.OrderTrial` sends a player army through woods and rocks for three minutes and reports order latency, stands and drops; `AgentPlay.StallTrial` does it for AI-vs-AI (counts path requests by unit and order). StallTrial also checks every move goal of the match against the unit's island (naming the commander point it came from), tags each stand with what came just before it (a target let go, a NavMesh rebuild, a new goal) and ends a stand where its unit died (counted to the end of the window, a rifleman killed while standing read as a 124 s "pending" stand; two earlier 93/111 s ones are consistent with it, unchecked). **Target: 0 goals cut off and no long stands** (map 2090223571: 0 of 2,510 goals; 0-7 stands of 1.2-1.9 s a run, mostly an attack-mover waiting out its 1.2 s re-path after a NavMesh rebuild took its path -- a known residual).
- **Send units only where they can get to: `NearestReachable`, never `NearestWalkable`.** `NearestWalkable` (`NavMesh.SamplePosition`) is the nearest NavMesh anywhere, and 17-41% of a generated map's ground NavMesh is islands no ground unit reaches (plateau tops, clearings ringed by trees and boulders); a unit sent there stands at the end of a partial path, on attack-move asking again every 1.2 s. `NavIslands` labels the connected pieces by walking Detour's own polygon graph (`Unity.AI.Navigation.LowLevel.NavWorld`: `MapLocation` under each polygon of `NavMesh.CalculateTriangulation`, then `GetEdgesAndNeighbors`), for `GroundAreas` and with Rubble (Maulers); rebuilding links from the triangulation's shared edges got carved footprints and tile borders wrong. Marked stale by NavMesh rebuilds and by structures/ore carving or leaving, rebuilt only when an answer depends on it, at most every 2 s (`Islands.Build()` forces it). `CmdMove` puts every goal on the unit's own island (which also takes a goal out of a structure's footprint), and the Commander and MechBrain snap their own points to their base's island. Checks: `AgentPlay.IslandReport` (labels against `NavMesh.CalculatePath`: 0 mismatches), `IslandBatch` (the AI's candidate points on ten maps, old way and new: 0 cut off the new way), StallTrial's goal audit. `ReachabilityOffSwitch` restores the old snapping for an A/B and is reset when play stops. **A trained unit exits onto the ground the bases share** (`Unit.UpdateProduction`: an exit spot `OnMainGround` first, any free walkable spot only failing that): the first walkable spot round a building can be a pocket it closes off against a cliff or trees (1370439406: an 81 m² patch, every unit trained there cut off; StallTrial reads them "unit cut off"). `OnMainGround` judges, and the unit is put down at, the ground NavMesh under the ring point (a point just off the mesh between a pocket and the main ground could read as the one and land in the other), and refreshes the islands first if a structure or ore field has carved within 40 m of it since the last build (`Invalidate(carvedAt)` from `Spawn`; that carve is what closes a pocket, and a stale "joined" answer is otherwise trusted; any carve at all made the next unit trained anywhere rebuild them, 23 -> 48 builds a match on seed 1000; within `Refresh`'s limits, not inside 2 s of the last build nor during a NavMesh rebuild). The fallback exit is put down on the ground NavMesh too (raw, it could warp onto a trunk's or boulder's Rubble). `GameWorld.ExitsMovedOffPocket` counts productions moved off a pocket (StallTrial prints it). Check `AgentPlay.ExitPocket` (a Garrison on main ground whose rally-facing exits lie in a pen of carving walls closed against its own footprint, the pen raised first so the Garrison's carve closes it; a rifleman trained under the old rule via `ReachabilityOff`, then the new): **target old in the pen with a partial path, new outside with a complete one and +1 moved** (measured on 2694109903, 1000 and 1370439406: old in the pen and cut off all three, new out at -36/-36/45 degrees, complete, +1 each). An AI match rarely forms such a pocket (0 in the last five StallTrials), so the trial is the evidence. **A point more than an agent's radius (0.6 m) off the NavMesh resolves to the main ground when that is within `OffMeshSlack` (1 m) as near** (`NavIslands.IslandAt`): a side's start position and the AI's `home` are the centre of a Foundry's carved hole, equally near the rim on every side, and a sliver the Foundry closes off must not decide where they stand (StallTrial prints "start positions on the main ground"); nearer than that a point keeps its own island (an agent on an island's edge can fail the triangle test by round-off). StallTrial also checks every trained unit with `NavMesh.CalculatePath` to the enemy base, independent of `NavIslands`.
- Water is not baked: the lake bed is ground (units wade up to `MapBuilder.WadeDepth`); deeper water is Not Walkable volumes on `Map/DeepWater`. Placement and walkability queries use `GameWorld.GroundAreas`; structures need dry ground.

## Vegetation and craters
`Vegetation` holds the plants MapBuilder placed (serialized `Plant` structs, per-kind meshes/colours) and their runtime state: felling, fire, burning out. Ticked by `GameWorld`, which also owns `Blast(...)` (what an explosion does to rocks, plants, ground; called from splash hits and `Kill`). Drawing: `View/VegetationRenderer` (GPU-instanced per kind, `SF_Tree`, remembers each plant as the player last saw it). `GroundDeformer` (added by `GameWorld.Awake`) digs craters into a runtime copy of the TerrainData; units ride the unchanged NavMesh, so `UnitView` sinks models by `Drop()`, and ground clutter, boulders, plants move by `LastChange()` on `Deformed`. Craters lower the ground only to the deepest single bowl over it (`GroundDeformer.Crater`); the blasted look is `GroundMask` channel A in `SF_Terrain`.
`Vegetation` state that needs the map (grass fuel, wetness) is built in `EnsureGrass` on the first tick, **not `Awake`** (`MapInfo.Instance` may not be set yet).
Trees close `PlantKind.blockRadius` (trunk + low boughs + a unit's radius) as Rubble; groves that would disconnect a base, expansion or ore field are dropped (`MapGenerator.Blockage`). MapChecks tests a ring 0.4 m outside each trunk and base-to-base connectivity. Undergrowth kinds set `drawDistance` and `castShadows = false`. **Plant kind indices are MapGenerator's constants: append new kinds, never reorder.**

## Felling
- **Blasts fell trees by chance, not radius** (`Vegetation.Blast`): the roll falls off with distance over `radius * 1.35` and with trunk thickness; odds scale with `0.38 / trunkRadius`, so a kind's `trunkRadius` is gameplay as well as the blocking ring. `AgentPlay.FellTrial` shells a wood 60 times and prints the rate per distance band: aim for ~80/35/17/8% out to 4.3 m for a Mauler's shell (with scanned trunks, broadleaf 0.48 m / jacaranda 0.40: 66/45/21/6%, 27% overall on a generated map).
- **A felled tree** (`Vegetation.Topple`) is a rod pivoting on its stump: angular acceleration `1.5 g sin(theta) / L` (slow start, faster later, tall takes longer; 1.5-1.7 s when a blast throws it). `Fell(..., push)` = shove in rad/s (blast 0.5-1.3 by distance; a burned-through snag 0.05, then downwind). It rests on its boughs (`Live.rest`), bounces once or twice; the rebound is capped at 0.45 rad/s (a tree flung down at 6 rad/s sprang back 45 degrees and fell again). `LodgeCheck` hangs it up in a neighbour's crown if one stands in the way (`Live.lodged`, `slipAt`) until it slips or that tree goes. `GameEvent.speed` carries the crown's impact speed, scaling dust, debris and camera shake.
- **A Mauler shoulders a tree over** (`Vegetation.CrushUnderMaulers`), kinematic contact not a shove: when the trunk meets the hull's nose box (`HullFront` 2.2, `HullSide` 1.6 m, from the model's track runs) it is `Fell` with no push and `Live.pushedBy` the tank; while held its lean is at least `atan(past / GlacisHeight)` (`past` = how far the nose is beyond the trunk's foot), so the trunk never enters the hull; gravity then drops it ahead. The blow multiplies `agent.velocity` by 0.3-0.7 by trunk radius; `Unit.pushLoad` (reset per tick, the roots' resistance) cuts `Unit.UpdateFacing`'s pace by up to 75%, works the engine (`FXDirector.MaulerEffects`) and lifts the nose (`UnitView`). Slowing only top speed let the tank lose nothing before the tree was over. A trunk under the footprint is pressed in (`Live.crush`, sinking up to 0.9 trunk radii) and raises `GameEventKind.PlantCrushed` (splinters, `bank.crush`). `AgentPlay.PushLook` drives one into a lone blocking tree and prints lean vs hull position, speed and the closest the trunk came to the glacis (measured: 4.1 m/s down to 1.4-2.0, down 1.4-1.8 s after contact, gap never below 0.27 m; `Temp/push_*.png`).

## Wind (`Wind.cs`)
One heading, strength and gust rhythm for the whole match, drawn from the map seed in `MapGenerator.Generate`, evaluated as a pure function of the match clock (no state; a seed replays identically). Everything that should agree reads it: fire spreads downwind and faster in a gust, smoke/embers lean with it, a falling tree leans with it, leaves tear off crowns when it blows hard (`FXDirector.WindBlown`), cloud shadows drift with it, `Atmosphere` uploads `_SF_Wind` (xy heading, z strength, w gust phase) for SF_Tree/SF_Grass (both fall back to a steady breeze when it is zero, so nothing stands frozen in the scene view). Sway = per-material `_WindStrength` x that strength. **Clouds are the exception**: they drift steadily with `Wind.Weather` (the day's strength) at `Atmosphere.cloudDrift` (3.2 m/s on the windiest day); driven by `Wind.Speed` at 14 m/s they surged and stalled with every gust ("wind is too fast"). Gust phase advances at `0.45 + 0.95 x speed` (`0.5 + 1.4 x` thrashed on a windy day).

## Fire (`Vegetation.Ignite/Spread/TickGrass`)
- Every kind burns at `PlantKind.burns`; spread runs downwind (`Vegetation.Wind`); each blaze draws a `vigour` its spread inherits, so most fires die in a couple of plants and a few run; vigorous ones throw embers downwind across gaps. Grass carries fire between stands: a fuel grid (`GrassCell` 3 m) built on the first tick, burning cell to cell and lighting plants it reaches.
- **Fire burns only where grass grows.** Fuel comes from `MapGenerator.GrassChance` (the rule GroundScatter places tufts by: the terrain's splat weights with the same water/slope/boulder cut-outs, looked at in nine 1 m squares a cell, `grassMask`). A cell burns only if `MinExpectedTufts` (2.5) tufts are expected at full density; flames stand only on its grown squares (`GrassTuft`); SF_Terrain lays ash only on the lichen layer. (The old fuel — meadow weight + a quarter of gravel — burned 4,051 cells, 1,661 bare; now 1,783, 4 bare. `AgentPlay.GrassFuelCheck` counts tufts per burnable cell and sweeps the threshold.) Drawn tufts are random and thinner on Balanced/Battery, so the match cannot be tuft-for-tuft; **the simulation must not depend on the preset.** Grass under structures and ore seams is cleared from fuel (`ClearGrassUnder`, from `GameWorld.Spawn` and `EnsureGrass`).
- **Grid fields are `[NonSerialized]`:** entering play mode the editor round-trips private fields, turning a null array into an empty one that looked built and indexed -1.
- Damp resists: `Wetness` (water level against ground at the spot and 5 m round it) cuts a cell's fuel and a plant's catch chance, so shores and reed beds barely take.
- Limits are per fire: each blaze takes at most `blazeCapPlants`/`blazeCapCells` (a sixth of the map's growth), so one fire cannot burn everything but a match's fires together can; `MaxBurning` (45) plants and `MaxGrassFires` (150) cells at once. Burnt grass never comes back: `GroundMask` uploads the grid as `_SF_BurntGrass` (SF_Grass: stubble and char; SF_Terrain: ash), which does not heal like the mask's burn.
- `AgentPlay.FireTrial`/`FireTrialReport` light 30 fires at 8x and report plants and grass cells each: aim for a median of a few plants, a long tail, most of the map gone after 30.
- **Structures catch from wildfire** (`GameWorld.StructureFires`, `Unit.burnUntil`): while a burning plant's crown or burning grass is within `FireReach` (1.5 m) of a footprint, every `FireCheck` (0.5 s) rolls `1 - exp(-CatchRate * heat * 0.5)` (`Vegetation.FireNear` gives heat). A fire reaching a wall delivers 7-24 heat-seconds, so at `CatchRate` 0.025 about 29% set it alight; **judge a change by that expected share (`AgentPlay.BuildingFireTrial` prints it), not the caught-count, which over 8-16 fires is noise.** Alight: burns 13-19 s for 6-12% of health, never below 1 hp (fire alone does not destroy a building), then safe for 45 s. `StructureIgnited` drives the alert, flare, `FXDirector.BuildingFire` and the fire bed in `AudioDirector`. The flame sprite's base is the bottom of its quad, so tongues stand on the top of the middle and low on the outer walls (stepped rounds: at full height over the lower ring they hung in the air; the building hides tongues inside its footprint; meshes are not readable in the player, so the roof cannot be sampled). `AgentPlay.BuildingFireLook` photographs (`Temp/building_burn_*.png`).
- Burning plants draw tongues from the `fx_flame.png` flipbook (`FXDirector.MakeFlames`, vertical billboards, `startSize3D` for tall quads; `EmitFlame` sizes are in the old sheet's units, a tongue half its quad: see `../View/CLAUDE.md`). Smouldering after a fire goes out is tracked in FXDirector, not the simulation.


## Morale (`GameWorld.Morale.cs`)
- **Rare by design.** Every 2 s each side is read; it is in a crisis only when at once: its army is under `Losing` 70% of the enemy's (`SideStrength`, Mechs left out -- counted in, a live Mech kept its side over the line until the army was gone, so the sides that broke had always lost their Mech and it never judged them; the fight's own odds below still count a Mech standing there); it lost `LostMin` 300+ and `BadExchange` 1.5x what it dealt in the last minute (`losses`, fed by `Kill`); 2+ of its soldiers were hit within 30 m of each other in the last 6 s, outgunned `Outgunned` 1.4:1 round them, 3+ of its soldiers there; and 2+ of them died within 40 m in 30 s. Each crisis gets **one** roll (`BreakChance` x 0.6-1.4 by how lopsided the war is); none before 300 s; at most `MaxEpisodes` (1) a side a match; ~15% of that fight run (1-4), never rank 2+, Diggers or the dying (under 25% hull). **Who breaks** (`BreakWeight`): riflemen before crews, the hurt before the whole, the green before a stripe, **the rear of the knot before its front** (x0.25 with an armed enemy within 10 m up to x1.75 with none within 35 m; unarmed buildings, Diggers and deserters do not count, or a knot by their base was all "front") **and not the one being shot this instant** (hit in the last 1.5 s: x0.4). Picked from the wounded under fire, 4 of 5 natural deserters were shot 0-2 s after the warning, never having got clear (the dying filter alone did not stop it). `LegacyBreakPick` is the old pick for the A/B (runners reset it). A crisis whose soldiers there are all ineligible spends its roll with no break: `unfitBreaks` counts it, and the evaluation line prints it; `lastRoll`/`lastOdds` keep the last crisis's roll for trials. Own `Rng` from the match seed. `MoraleState.gates` counts which gate stopped each reading (the evaluation line prints them). At 55% / 400 / 2x the war gates passed only once the army was already gone (Full eval: 3006 of 4294 losing-side readings stopped at strength), so the decisive fight came before the side counted as losing: 0 desertions in 20 matches. With the war gates at 70% / 300 / 1.5x a crisis came 3 times in 20 matches, and at `BreakChance` 0.25 none broke. **Target: a desertion in about 1 Full-eval match in 10, wins at baseline.** With Mechs counted in the war and `BreakChance` 0.6: 2 of 20 (8 crises), both sides' Mechs already lost. With Mechs left out: 12 crises and 4 of 20 at 0.6; `BreakChance` 0.4: 3 of 20 (12 crises), then 2 of 20 (5 crises); wins 13-15/20 (one loss in one run, a match with no desertion on either side). Judge the rate by crises x the roll, not one 20-match count. **The evaluation's 20 matches share five seeds** (`1000 + 37 x game`, the same against every opponent), and `moraleRng` came from the seed alone: their first two draws were all above the 0.35 most a crisis can roll at 0.25, so no evaluation match could break before its third crisis (0 in 40 at 0.25 was that, not the rules; every count above was drawn on those shared sequences too). `ReseedMorale`, called by `AIEvalRunner` at the start, mixes the opponent in; a played match never calls it. **Final `BreakChance` 0.25, rolls per opponent: 2 of 20 (13 crises), 13/20 wins; 2 of 20 (4 crises), 16/20; with the dying left out, 1 of 20 (7 crises), 15/20; with the rear-first pick, 1 of 20 (6 crises), 15/20; 0 losses and 0 exceptions in all four** -- 6 in 80 (about 1 in 13), each a lone soldier; before the rear-first pick 4 of 5 died 0-2 s after breaking, the one since 5 s after (its side was being routed, lost at 429 s). One natural desertion an evaluation is too few to judge survival: `BreakPick` is the measure. `NaturalChain` shows the whole chain unforced. Any other per-match stream seeded from the match seed alone repeats across the evaluation's opponents the same way.
- **Only in a real match:** `GameBootstrap.OnTick` sets `moraleOn = !Commander.Suspended`, so trials with a silenced opponent (Mech duels, bay edge cases) never break; morale trials force it with `Desert(t, at, group, count)`.
- **The camp** is the reachable point nearest the map corner behind the base if it is `CampNear` 40-`CampFar` 85 m from the base's middle, `CampClear` 25 m from the side's structures and dry; else rings from the corner's bearing, and failing those the candidate furthest from the side's structures; if none of those was dry and in range, on round the far side, the bearing nearest the corner's first, on it the radius clearest of its structures (left at the raw corner point it could be in the base or the water; ranked by clearance alone it went toward the enemy) (`campFromHome`/`campClearance` record where it came out). Bases sit 40-46 m from the walkable corner on many maps (cliff rims), so CampNear cannot be raised much; a 55 m minimum sent camps 60 degrees round to a map edge, no corner at all. `NavIslands` refresh at most every 2 s: a check that regenerates maps must call `Islands.Build()` per map (asked in one frame, 40 seeds read the first map's islands).
