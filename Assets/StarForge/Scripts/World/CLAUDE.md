## Simulation
`GameWorld` (`[DefaultExecutionOrder(-50)]`) owns all match state and ticks every `Unit` itself in `Update`, `dt` clamped to 0.1 s: cache positions → rebuild spatial hash → `Unit.Tick` → compact dead units, rebuild hash → projectiles → visibility (every 0.12 s) → supply → win check → `Ticked`. `Unit` = order logic on a `NavMeshAgent`; `UnitView` = presentation only and **must never write to the `Unit`**. Gameplay effects leave the world only as `GameWorld.Event` (`GameEvent`), consumed by `FXDirector`, `AudioDirector`, `GroundMask`, `HUDController`.
Anything needing its own randomness uses its own stream seeded from the match seed (`fireRng`, wreck directions from the unit id), never the match's stream, or a seeded match shifts.
**Kept state that must be told when its source changes** (else it goes stale with no error): anything writing the visibility grid (`vis`/`explored`; only `UpdateVisibility` does) bumps `GameWorld.VisVersion`, which `FogOfWarRenderer` skips frames on; every path into `units` (`BeginMatch`, `Spawn`) adds a Foundry to `foundries`, which `NearestDropoff` reads; a map generated in place mid-play goes through `Vegetation.PlantsReplaced` (`MapGenerator.Generate` calls it; before it, trials that regenerated indexed past `live` every frame). Check: `MathChecks.Run`/`Report` (Editor, play mode, ~50 s), 0 mismatches against the full maths and 0 exceptions.
**No managed garbage in per-frame paths, and `UnityEngine.Object.name` is garbage** (a new string each read: `Material.name` in `UnitView.ApplySurface` was 105 allocations a frame, `AnimationClip.name` in `Fauna` 30; cache a clip's name). **`ApplySurface` tells a unit's own material by reference** (a slot not copied holds the very material in `sharedMats`); the `" (unit)"` name is still how `DropOwnMaterials`, `OwnCopies`, `SurfaceFault` and `SurfaceTrials` tell a copy, once, off the per-frame path. Rebuilt scratch collections are garbage too (`NavIslands.Build` keeps its own; what a build still allocates, ~5.5 KB a frame on average, is the triangulation and the arrays it keeps: corners, heights, labels, the lookup grid). Measure by call site, not by guess: in play, `ProfilerDriver.memoryRecordMode = ProfilerMemoryRecordMode.GCAlloc`, record, then for each `GC.Alloc` sample `RawFrameDataView.GetSampleCallstack` + `ResolveMethodInfo` (first `StarForge` frame and line; a `Unity_RunCommand` can do it). (records: README › World: Simulation)

## Tactical combat (`GameWorld.Tactical.cs`, `SightMap`, `Unit.Tactical.cs`, `Sim/TacticalRules`)
The game's combat, shooter-style (the tactical rewrite, phases 0-8). **The old combat and its switch were deleted at phase 8 (2026-11-20)**: `TacticalRules.On`, `GameWorld.Tactical`, `-sftactical`/`-sfclassic`, `AIEvalRunner.EvalClassic`, the menu's Rules chip and setup row, `UnitDef.tacticalOnly` and the old visibility pass; git history before that commit has them. `Defs` hands out copies of the unit assets with `TacticalRules.Apply`'s numbers, so what reads `def.range` needs no change and `Data/Units/*.asset` keep the old numbers. Done so far: all infantry's guns, thinking, rigged bodies, squads, side arms and magazines, the Gunner, wrecks and tanks as cover, mistakes by experience, the map's low walls, ruins and ditches with vaulting, fallen trunks and craters as cover, and everyone's sight; then tanks, creatures, the Mech and aircraft (phases 6-7, below).
- **A soldier's own judgement** (`Unit.Soldier.cs`, `def.bodyRadius` > 0, four times a second by match time, never by the camera). **Suppression**: a round passing within 2.5 m adds up to 0.06, half that in cover; a hit 0.12, a burst 0.5; it ebbs 0.2 a second once 1.5 s pass with none; it widens his own aim up to x2.2. **Courage** (`Courage`): a hash of his id, 0.12 a rank, 0.05 a friend within 15 m up to four, -0.15 a friend down within 20 m in 8 s up to three. **Pinned** at `0.45 + 0.45 x courage`, flat, up at 0.6 of that; under 0.5 courage and well past it he may roll **panic** every 6 s: 2-4 s frozen or bolting for cover, whatever the order. **Cover** (`FindCover`): rocks, thick trunks, walls and folds within 12 m, judged against the nearest threat and where the enemy stands as a whole, only spots sheltering over 0.6 of him; a spot he can shoot from counts for more; looks are rationed at 20 a second of match time. He runs at 6 m/s without firing, then in cover leans out for his round and ducks back between rounds (`ShowsForShot`, `leanDir`: what shows of him to anyone is the side he leans to), hides when it gets hot, moves on if flanked, and on an attack bounds onward from cover to cover. **Stopped in the open more than 15 m from the enemy he lies down.** Speeds: walk 3.5, run 6, crouched x0.65, crawling x0.27.
- **Orders come first** (`ReadOrder`): a new order takes him out of his cover at once; an order running out to Idle, or the same one asked again within `NewOrderFrom` 6 m (the AI repeats its orders every pass), does not, and a repeat does not walk him out of it. Pinned, a Move is obeyed at a crawl until it eases. On Hold he only changes posture. **`Damage`'s "idle defenders retaliate" is off for soldiers**: it turned Idle into Attack and every man in cover bounded forward when first hit. A deserter stands down (`StandDown`), or he keeps a run's or a crawl's pace.
- **The rigged body** (`SoldierRigView`, `UnitView.Bind`): the rigid parts stay under the unit, hidden; from his feet he falls as the old ragdoll (they come back for `UnitWreck`, only if the death is seen); lying flat he stays as he lay (`SoldierBody`, 7 s, then sinks). Flash, damage and burn go on a material of his own, written on change: a property block would take him out of the SRP Batcher. A cape on the hidden body goes down with him, cloth off while he is down (`InfantryCape.WearerDown`).
- **`Fight` does not reach its target on today's maps.** Six among cover against eight over open ground, both thinking, map 1000, 12 fixed fields (`UseSeed1000` first, once per Play; one field never makes contact): the six won 2 of 12 (target 4 of 5), both plain 4 of 12, and those plain fights last 12-29 s against 21-51 s. In those fights rounds at the six in cover struck 13-40%, about 22%, and rounds at the eight about the same, so 8 rifles' extra fire decides it. **Why:** to shoot past a boulder a man stands at its edge, open to whoever is further round on that side, and a boulder is too tall to fire over; on `Cover`'s frontal staging it halves the hits (5/28, 7/28, 5/28 against 17/55, 22/56, 12/28). Low walls to fire over came in phase 5: what the six do behind them is `TacticalTrials.FightWalls` (below), whose target the user set at about half (2026-11-20).
- **A small-arms round is decided when fired** (`FireShot`, `DecideShot`; `UnitDef.shotSpread` > 0): a normal error about the aim from `combatRng`, then the first thing on the line: ground half a metre at a time, boulders, trunks (`SightMap.FirstStop`), structures of either side, enemies; **friends it flies past**. Range, posture and cover have no numbers of their own: a far or low body is a small target and cover stops the round. The tracer (`Tracer`, `Projectile.cosmetic`) is only what is seen, kind 0 as before. Shots at aircraft stay real rounds. A round is as heavy as before on all that is no soldier: `Apply` scales every class by old/new damage and `vsSoldier` undoes that on a soldier (`ShotDamage`; `vsMech` multiplies on top of `vsHeavy`, so it stays alone).
- **A soldier shoots at what shows** (`ShotAim`): the body's middle, or the head and shoulders over a rock too high for that, and **only with a way there** (`LineOfFire`, kept 0.25 s, 0.5 s while blocked, from the muzzle as if already turned on the target: which way he faces now says nothing). Out of reach or with no way, free to choose he takes another a round would reach, what fights back first and, closing on what fights back, only another that does (`NearestShootable`, `FireBlocked`, twice a second: the old give-way rule). Closing he walks up; **a longer way round that gets there he walks; a partial path with three seconds and no metre gained he gives up**, and for 30 s takes only what a round would reach (`lineOnlyUntil`): an attack-move walks on, an attack order ends (the path's status is the test, a request in flight says nothing, and "stopped" is no test since a partial path keeps a unit walking). **No gun fires at what its side does not know of**, and an order after a unit that has gone from sight goes to where it was last known (`targetSeenAt`) and no further. Any gun's shot gives its owner away (`RevealShooter`).
- **Squads and what they do together** (`GameWorld.Squads.cs`, no UI, nothing to train; soldiers only, `def.bodyRadius` > 0). Men sent together are one, in eights, grouped by who stands near whom; a loose man joins the squad whose **nearest man** is within 15 m (by the centre, eight in a 2.5 m line split 6 + 2), and past 30 m from the centre he leaves. **A squad all of it sent, or sent where the rest of it is already going, stays the same object with what it remembers** (`GoingThere`; rebuilt, the AI's repeated orders wiped `reportedAt` and `lostAt` every pass), as does one in contact when part of it is sent after a target (the Commander's focus fire); only the part sent elsewhere leaves. **An order's squad does not merge back for 10 s, and two merge only going the same way or both idle** (`SameWay`; sent two of five off, they rejoined on the next pass). **Contact** is fire near a man in the last 4 s, a man not Free, or a target that fights back, seen, within 40 m (a Digger or a wall is no contact: a plain Move past one went at half speed). **Turns** (`TakeTurns`, every 0.5 s, 4.5 s a turn): each man keeps his half (`fireTeam`, rebalanced only when lopsided); out of turn a walker stops (`StopForTurn`), over the half too; a man already running for cover is left alone and a **flanked** man moves out of turn. Closing, the re-path, the Move's `KeepGoing` and `LookForCover` all ask `SquadLetsMove`. **A stop says nothing about the way**: `FireBlocked` does not count a man out of turn toward giving up (a 4.5 s turn against 3 s would have ended Attack orders), and **`resumeMove`** restarts a stopped Move (`KeepGoing` reads an agent with no path as arrived). The same order asked again does not set an out-of-turn man walking (`MayWalkNow`). One half (`fireTeam` 1) works round after 6 s in contact (`FlankPull`: in its cover scoring, and `ClosingGoal` 12 m to the side when closing). **Point man:** on an attack-move out of contact, in squads of 3+, those not nearest their goal walk x0.8 within 12 m of him; a man alone goes crouched.
- **A plain Move is careful, a hurried one is not** (`Unit.Careful`, `hurry`: the user's "right-click lets units choose the approach"). In contact a soldier on Move takes targets but **shoots only while stopped and closes on nothing**, keeps low (on his way he crouches rather than crawls), bounds from cover to cover **nearer his goal** (`toGoal` in `FindCoverSpot`, at least 1 m gained a bound), and in cover within `CoverIsThere` 6 m of it he is there. `ReadOrder`'s repeat rule now covers Move: the same place asked again does not bring him out of cover unless hurried. **Hurry** (double right-click within 0.35 s and 3 m, a parameter of `CmdMove`/`CmdAttack`/`CmdSmart`): runs at 6 m/s, no cover, no turns, out of cover at once; pinned he stays down; clears at Idle or Hold, and any later order without it clears it. The Commander hurries a squad that reports it must fall back (phase 8, below); its own retreats are hurried too since 2026-11-22 (`Commander.LegacyWaveEnd` puts the plain Move back; `../AI/CLAUDE.md`).
- **Reports** (`Report`, `MakeReport`, `GameEventKind.SquadReport`, `HUDController.SquadLine`): text in the comms feed with a radio chirp, no accept; a click centres and selects those still with us (`PlayerController.SelectUnits`; all gone, the selection stays). **A line says who and where** (`Squad.callsign` Alpha..Zulu: twelve came round again while the first squads lived, `GameEvent.who`; "4 men 150 m north-east of our base", `Whereabouts`), and **with the pointer over it its men are ringed** (`PlayerController.HighlightUnits`, `Highlighted`) and the map pinged; leaving the line or its going clears them, if it is the line that rang them (an older line dropped while a newer one was pointed at cleared the newer one's rings). **FallBack** when a third of the squad (at least 2) fell in 20 s, or 1.6x outvalued with half of it pinned; **NeedAir** when 8 s in contact with something heavy or a structure that fights back, no armed aircraft within 50 m; losses come first, then NeedAir, then outvalued, and news already given falls through to the next; **an outvalued call waits while a NeedAir can still come** (a heavy threat, no armed aircraft near, under 8 s in contact): first, it used up the squad's 30 s and a squad held by a Mauler said "fall back" instead of asking for aircraft. Limits: 30 s a squad, 10 s a side, 60 s per place and kind within 40 m. It is raised for both sides, and **the Commander acts on its own side's** (`Commander.HearSquad`, queued and heard after its reaction time, never while silenced: fall back moves the men 35 m toward home, hurried, unless a wave or a feint is out (its own retreat decides then) or they are within 55 m of home; need air sends up to three idle armed aircraft that hurt armour to attack-move there; paid from its APM budget).
- Checks (`TacticalTrials`, Editor, map random per play; the 2026-11-04 readings: README › World: Tactical combat): `Squads` (eight attack-moving at six: over half moving for more than 0.3 s in under 5% of contact frames is the target; four against ten: FallBack, a click on the line selects those alive; four held by a Mauler: NeedAir; **flanking is slight on open ground**), `SquadWalk` (**sent after one 38 m off, 0 of 8 lost the order or target in 20 s**), `Orders` (**Move from cover under three rifles left at 0.1 s but pinned and 21 m off after 25 s**: hurry is the answer, and only suppressing them unpins him), `Match`, `CoverGoal` (target under 2 m, not met), `Restart`, `Cost` (sight rules target under 0.5 ms a frame), `Walls`, `FightWide` (target 4 of 5, still missed; phase 5's low walls were judged by `TacticalTrials.FightWalls`, below).
- **What a soldier carries besides his gun** (`Unit.SideArms.cs`, `GameWorld.Grenades.cs`, numbers in `TacticalRules`): Trooper pistol, knife, frag x2, smoke x1, flash x1; the other infantry pistol, knife, frag x1, smoke x1; nobody orders any of it. **Magazines, never short of ammunition** (`UnitDef.magazine`/`reloadTime`, set only by `Apply`; `Unit.rounds`, `SpendRound`, `BeginReload`): Trooper 20 rounds and 2 s with the cooldown at 0.6 (0.7 before: the same fire over a magazine and its reload, so the numbers above still hold; **the reload starts after the last round's own wait**, `BeginReload`: overlapped, the Marksman fired 19% faster than before, the Rocketeer 12%), Marksman 5 and 3 s (cooldown 2.6), Thumper and Rocketeer 1 (the reload is the old cooldown), Scorcher a 40-tick tank and 2.5 s (about 17% less jet over time: judge it at phase 8), Gunner a 40-round belt and 4 s. Only at men and light things: armour and walls get the main gun or nothing. **The knife** is arm's length (1.7 m to the edge, with a way there; he stands and uses it rather than run for cover; it does not give him away). **A close target with no way there is not the end of him** (`CloseQuarters` returns false without a line): he looks for what else he can shoot, or a mortar for what is in its band.
- **Throwing** (`TryThrow`, once a second, 0.5 s wind-up, one man a squad at a time, 6 s; 15 s after smoke). **Frag** at men, 9-24 m, at what his rifle cannot reach (`noLine`, only of his own target) or two or more within 5 m, on a 50% roll. **Flash** goes first when going in on men he has no line to, 9.5-18 m. **Smoke** pinned, or caught in the open with no cover found, over 0.3 suppression, thrown toward them. **A frag is safe by where friends will be when it bursts**: `FriendsClear` at reach + `ThrowErrorMax` over flight + fuse, asked at the decision and again as it leaves his hand, and **his squad does not move up until it has gone off** (`fragUntil`, `TakeTurns` stops them). It flies a solved time to a point up to 2.5 m off (6% of the range), `OffWhatStands` rolls it off a rock or wall (10-21% landed inside a rock at those ranges, and a burst inside one was smothered for everyone), lands, and goes off 1 s later. Men within 7 m get away from it on a 75% roll (`DiveFrom`, not the pinned rule's: lying flat where it gets him, which is no cover). **Burst** (`FragBurst`): 110 x (1 - distance / 6 m), so dead within about 2.5 m; x0.12 with a rock, trunk or wall in the way (`RoundGets`), x0.6 lying flat, x0.35 on light vehicles, x0.05 on armour and walls. **Flash** (`FlashBurst`): within 6.5 m with a way there, 1.25-2.5 s frozen and noticing nothing (`Stun`, `blindUntil`, `Notices`); an order waits for him (`Stunned`). **Restock** one item every 10 s within 20 m of a finished Foundry, Garrison or Bunkhouse.

## Navigation areas
- Boulders (`Boulder.cs`) and trunks of trees on walkable ground are left out of the bake and stand on the `Rubble` area (`NavMeshModifierVolume`s; trees' all sit on `Map/Vegetation`); every agent except the Mauler excludes it. `GameWorld` crushes a rock and `Vegetation` fells a tree when a Mauler reaches it or a blast does, then `GameWorld.RequestNavRebuild` rebuilds the tiles (`NavMeshSurface.UpdateNavMesh`, at most every 1.5 s: `navRebuildNotBefore`). `GameWorld.Awake` swaps in a copy of the baked data first, so a match never edits the asset; it also sets `NavMesh.pathfindingIterationsPerFrame` to 1000 (Unity's 100 let a rebuild restart long requests: Maulers in a wood waited up to 9 s).

## Vegetation and craters
`Vegetation` holds the plants MapBuilder placed (serialized `Plant` structs, per-kind meshes/colours) and their runtime state: felling, fire, burning out. Ticked by `GameWorld`, which also owns `Blast(...)` (what an explosion does to rocks, plants, ground; called from splash hits and `Kill`). Drawing: `View/VegetationRenderer` (GPU-instanced per kind, `SF_Tree`, remembers each plant as the player last saw it). `GroundDeformer` (added by `GameWorld.Awake`) digs craters into a runtime copy of the TerrainData; units ride the unchanged NavMesh, so `UnitView` sinks models by `Drop()`, and ground clutter, boulders, plants move by `LastChange()` on `Deformed`. Craters lower the ground only to the deepest single bowl over it (`GroundDeformer.Crater`); the blasted look is `GroundMask` channel A in `SF_Terrain`.
`Vegetation` state that needs the map (grass fuel, wetness) is built in `EnsureGrass` on the first tick, **not `Awake`** (`MapInfo.Instance` may not be set yet).
Trees close `PlantKind.blockRadius` (trunk + low boughs + a unit's radius) as Rubble; groves that would disconnect a base, expansion or ore field are dropped (`MapGenerator.Blockage`). MapChecks tests a ring 0.4 m outside each trunk and base-to-base connectivity. Undergrowth kinds set `drawDistance` and `castShadows = false`. **Plant kind indices are MapGenerator's constants: append new kinds, never reorder.**

## Felling
- **Blasts fell trees by chance, not radius** (`Vegetation.Blast`): the roll falls off with distance over `radius * 1.35` and with trunk thickness; odds scale with `0.38 / trunkRadius`, so a kind's `trunkRadius` is gameplay as well as the blocking ring. `AgentPlay.FellTrial` shells a wood 60 times and prints the rate per distance band: aim for ~80/35/17/8% out to 4.3 m for a Mauler's shell (with scanned trunks, broadleaf 0.48 m / jacaranda 0.40: 66/45/21/6%, 27% overall on a generated map).
- **A felled tree** (`Vegetation.Topple`) is a rod pivoting on its stump: angular acceleration `1.5 g sin(theta) / L` (slow start, faster later, tall takes longer; 1.5-1.7 s when a blast throws it). `Fell(..., push)` = shove in rad/s (blast 0.5-1.3 by distance; a burned-through snag 0.05, then downwind). It rests on its boughs (`Live.rest`), bounces once or twice; the rebound is capped at 0.45 rad/s (a tree flung down at 6 rad/s sprang back 45 degrees and fell again). `LodgeCheck` hangs it up in a neighbour's crown if one stands in the way (`Live.lodged`, `slipAt`) until it slips or that tree goes. `GameEvent.speed` carries the crown's impact speed, scaling dust, debris and camera shake.
- **A Mauler shoulders a tree over** (`Vegetation.CrushUnderMaulers`), kinematic contact not a shove: when the trunk meets the hull's nose box (`HullFront` 2.2, `HullSide` 1.6 m, from the model's track runs) it is `Fell` with no push and `Live.pushedBy` the tank; while held its lean is at least `atan(past / GlacisHeight)` (`past` = how far the nose is beyond the trunk's foot), so the trunk never enters the hull; gravity then drops it ahead. The blow multiplies `agent.velocity` by 0.3-0.7 by trunk radius; `Unit.pushLoad` (reset per tick, the roots' resistance) cuts `Unit.UpdateFacing`'s pace by up to 75%, works the engine (`FXDirector.MaulerEffects`) and lifts the nose (`UnitView`). Slowing only top speed let the tank lose nothing before the tree was over. A trunk under the footprint is pressed in (`Live.crush`, sinking up to 0.9 trunk radii) and raises `GameEventKind.PlantCrushed` (splinters, `bank.crush`). `AgentPlay.PushLook` drives one into a lone blocking tree and prints lean vs hull position, speed and the closest the trunk came to the glacis (measured: 4.1 m/s down to 1.4-2.0, down 1.4-1.8 s after contact, gap never below 0.27 m; `Temp/push_*.png`).

## Wind (`Wind.cs`)
One heading, strength and gust rhythm for the whole match, drawn from the map seed in `MapGenerator.Generate`, evaluated as a pure function of the match clock (no state; a seed replays identically). Everything that should agree reads it: fire spreads downwind and faster in a gust, smoke/embers lean with it, a falling tree leans with it, leaves tear off crowns when it blows hard (`FXDirector.WindBlown`), cloud shadows drift with it, `Atmosphere` uploads `_SF_Wind` (xy heading, z strength, w gust phase) for SF_Tree/SF_Grass (both fall back to a steady breeze when it is zero, so nothing stands frozen in the scene view). Sway = per-material `_WindStrength` x that strength. **Clouds are the exception**: they drift steadily with `Wind.Weather` (the day's strength) at `Atmosphere.cloudDrift` (3.2 m/s on the windiest day); driven by `Wind.Speed` at 14 m/s they surged and stalled with every gust ("wind is too fast"). Gust phase advances at `0.45 + 0.95 x speed` (`0.5 + 1.4 x` thrashed on a windy day).

## Fire (`Vegetation.Ignite/Spread/TickGrass`)
- Every kind burns at `PlantKind.burns`; spread runs downwind (`Vegetation.Wind`); each blaze draws a `vigour` its spread inherits, so most fires die in a couple of plants and a few run; vigorous ones throw embers downwind across gaps. Grass carries fire between stands: a fuel grid (`GrassCell` 3 m) built on the first tick, burning cell to cell and lighting plants it reaches.
- **Fire burns only where grass grows.** Fuel comes from `MapGenerator.GrassChance` (the rule GroundScatter places tufts by: the terrain's splat weights with the same water/slope/boulder cut-outs, looked at in nine 1 m squares a cell, `grassMask`). A cell burns only if `MinExpectedTufts` (2.5) tufts are expected at full density; flames stand only on its grown squares (`GrassTuft`); SF_Terrain lays ash only on the lichen layer. (The old fuel — meadow weight + a quarter of gravel — burned 4,051 cells, 1,661 bare; now 1,783, 4 bare. `AgentPlay.GrassFuelCheck` counts tufts per burnable cell and sweeps the threshold.) Drawn tufts are random and thinner on Balanced/Battery, so the match cannot be tuft-for-tuft; **the simulation must not depend on the preset.** Grass under structures and ore seams is cleared from fuel (`ClearGrassUnder`, from `GameWorld.Spawn` and `EnsureGrass`).
- **Grid fields are `[NonSerialized]`:** entering play mode the editor round-trips private fields, turning a null array into an empty one that looked built and indexed -1.
- Damp resists: `Wetness` (water level against ground at the spot and 5 m round it) cuts a cell's fuel and a plant's catch chance, so shores and reed beds barely take.
- Limits are per fire: each blaze takes at most `blazeCapPlants`/`blazeCapCells` (a sixth of the map's growth), so one fire cannot burn everything but a match's fires together can; `MaxBurning` (45) plants and `MaxGrassFires` (150) cells at once. Burnt grass never comes back: `GroundMask` uploads the grid as `_SF_BurntGrass` (SF_Grass: stubble and char; SF_Terrain: ash), which does not heal like the mask's burn.
- `AgentPlay.FireTrial`/`FireTrialReport` light 30 fires at 8x and report plants and grass cells each: aim for a median of a few plants, a long tail, most of the map gone after 30.
- **Structures catch from wildfire** (`GameWorld.StructureFires`, `Unit.burnUntil`): while a burning plant's crown or burning grass is within `FireReach` (1.5 m) of a footprint, every `FireCheck` (0.5 s) rolls `1 - exp(-CatchRate * heat * 0.5)` (`Vegetation.FireNear` gives heat). A fire reaching a wall delivers 7-24 heat-seconds, so at `CatchRate` 0.025 about 29% set it alight; **judge a change by that expected share (`AgentPlay.BuildingFireTrial` prints it), not the caught-count, which over 8-16 fires is noise.** Alight: burns 13-19 s for 6-12% of health, never below 1 hp (fire alone does not destroy a building), then safe for 45 s. `StructureIgnited` drives the alert, flare, `FXDirector.BuildingFire` and the fire bed in `AudioDirector`. The flame sprite's base is the bottom of its quad, so tongues stand on the top of the middle and low on the outer walls (since 2026-11-20 they stand on each structure's measured roof profile, `FXDirector.RoofOf`, baked from the FBXs by `Tools/blender/roof_profile.py` since the player cannot read the meshes: `../View/CLAUDE.md`, Fire on buildings). `AgentPlay.BuildingFireLook` photographs (`Temp/building_burn_*.png`).
- Burning plants draw tongues from the `fx_flame.png` flipbook (`FXDirector.MakeFlames`, vertical billboards, `startSize3D` for tall quads; `EmitFlame` sizes are in the old sheet's units, a tongue half its quad: see `../View/CLAUDE.md`). Smouldering after a fire goes out is tracked in FXDirector, not the simulation.


## Morale (`GameWorld.Morale.cs`)
- **Rare by design.** Every 2 s each side is read; it is in a crisis only when at once: its army is under `Losing` 70% of the enemy's (`SideStrength`, Mechs left out -- counted in, a live Mech kept its side over the line until the army was gone, so the sides that broke had always lost their Mech and it never judged them; the fight's own odds below still count a Mech standing there); it lost `LostMin` 300+ and `BadExchange` 1.5x what it dealt in the last minute (`losses`, fed by `Kill`); 2+ of its soldiers were hit within 30 m of each other in the last 6 s, outgunned `Outgunned` 1.4:1 round them, 3+ of its soldiers there; and 2+ of them died within 40 m in 30 s. Each crisis gets **one** roll (`BreakChance` x 0.6-1.4 by how lopsided the war is); none before 300 s; at most `MaxEpisodes` (1) a side a match; ~15% of that fight run (1-4), never rank 2+, Diggers or the dying (under 25% hull). **Who breaks** (`BreakWeight`): riflemen before crews, the hurt before the whole, the green before a stripe, **the rear of the knot before its front** (x0.25 with an armed enemy within 10 m up to x1.75 with none within 35 m; unarmed buildings, Diggers and deserters do not count, or a knot by their base was all "front") **and not the one being shot this instant** (hit in the last 1.5 s: x0.4).
