# Mechs: rules, parts, presentation — area notes

One Mech Bay and one Mech a side, a match (README "Mechs"). Mech *AI* (MechBrain, speech, doctrine, bay edge cases): `../../AI/CLAUDE.md`.

## Rules
- `World/GameWorld.Mech.cs`: the bay (drop timer `MechDropDelay` 120 s, self-repair, repairing the Mech, upgrades `CmdMechUpgrade`), the Mech's guns `FireMechGun`; `Faction.bay/mech/design/upgrades/researching`.
- `MechParts.cs`: fixed parts list, the 100-point budget, `Generate(seed)`, upgrade costs — the balance sheet.
- `MechCore.cs` (on the Mech's GameObject): stats from parts and upgrades, shield and armour in `Absorb`, every gun picks its own target.
- `Unit.Tick` hands a Mech to `TickMech`; `Unit.Untargetable` = a Mech still coming down. Designs are drawn in `BeginMatch` from the match seed, so a seed replays.
- **The whole Mech is scaled to an army's measure** (`MechParts.HullScale` 0.55: frame hull, shield capacity and regen, nanites, the bay's quiet repair; `GunScale` 0.28: every shot, through `MechCore.DamageMul`, so `Dps()` and MechBrain's odds agree). Target, the user's call (2026-10-28): **a bare Mech falls to 6-7 bare Maulers coming from 55 m** (at 1/1 it took 21 Maulers, 3,000 ore against a 100-ore bay, and matches came down to the two Mechs). Damage is cut harder than hull so squads are not erased and a Mech-vs-Mech fight lasts long enough for the armies to tip it. Measured (`MechDuel.RunBaseFreshAll`, twelve designs, fresh map): **6.3 Maulers (4-8), 10.2 Troopers (7-13; Troopers bisect to two, so up to one high)**; a Maulers-only pass 6.4; 0.45/0.24 gave 5.0, 0.55/0.32 7.2 (one pass of Maulers-only twelve designs is ~8 min). Fully upgraded, first six: 8.2 Maulers / 16.3 Troopers against bare 5.2 / 9.2 (1.59x / 1.78x; the Trooper ratio is over the 1.7 aim, within its noise: Troopers move ~20% per design between runs). **The upgrades are priced with the Mech** (`MechParts` upgrade costs: Servos 50/90, Armour and Overcharge 60/95/130, Hardpoint 120, Targeting 70: 900 in all, about twice the three Maulers' worth they add, as before; at 2,600 they bought a sixth of their price). **A Mech's army worth is one number, `Defs.ArmyValue(Mech)`** (950 = 6.3 Maulers; 3,200 before): `MechArmoury.Read` (and 0.56 of it for a bay not yet dropped), morale's loss and fight odds and `MechSpeech`'s worth, assault and standing (half of it; the standing's feeds `Mood`, hence `Punish`), MechBrain's threat and prey sums, the Commander's home threat and the survey's enemy-Mech mix read it; the influence weight is 19 (60 before). Re-derive it, `MechNeed` and MechBrain's strike-advice 750 from the duel whenever the duel numbers move. `MechArmoury.Score` weighs the hull before the scale, so picks against an army are unchanged (`ArmourySurvey` 375-385 loadouts, bodies 3-4%, flame 67/47%, laser or railgun 71/85%); against an enemy Mech, now a smaller part of the threat, 384 loadouts, flame 50%, laser or railgun 83% (379 / 46% / 85% at 3,200). `LegacyBalance` A/Bs run on the scaled Mech. **Open:** the bay was not scaled, and a finished bay dropped 44-66 m from an enemy base now usually holds where their Mech used to break it (BayEdgeCases cases 1 and 3; the Commander's home-defence answer and what else was tried in `../../AI/CLAUDE.md`).
- The bay mends at `MechCore.BayRepairRate` (22 x HullScale = 12.1/s once left alone 4 s: a Mech back at 38% needs about a minute) and `BayRepairUnderFire` 6 x **GunScale** = 1.7/s (sized with the guns: it keeps the share of an enemy Mech's fire it mends, which a hull-sized rate would have doubled). At 50/15 what an army had cost a Mech was gone in 20 s and a player's attack counted for nothing unless it finished the job.
- **A battered Mech fires slower** (`MechCore.Battered`, 0 above 60% hull, 1 at 15%: cooldowns up to `BatteredSlow` 45% longer). `Dps()` includes it, so MechBrain judges fights by what it can do now; the HUD card shows "Battle-damaged: firing N% slower" (fog-fair: the hull bar is in sight).
- **Upgrades are steps** (`MechParts.ArmourHullPerLevel` 0.05, `ArmourPerLevel` 0.02, `WeaponsPerLevel` 0.05; Extra Hardpoint one level; Targeting +6% range). At 10%/4/10%, two hardpoints and +12% range a fully upgraded Mech took 2.4x the army a bare one did and a player's army counted for little against it (MechDuel fresh-map A/B, six designs: full 40.8 Maulers / 100+ Troopers → 26.2 / 61.2; bare 17.2 / 44.7 → 16.8 / 36.8; upgrades now 1.56x / 1.66x, target ≤1.7x). Bare on all twelve designs before the scale (`RunBaseFreshNew` + `RunBaseFreshNewLastSix`, the last six mostly heavy frames at 24.5 / 43): **20.7 Maulers / 40 Troopers**: inside the Mauler target, at the Trooper ceiling (four of the last six designs are above 40, and in play the rotary cannon fires 10% more than in a duel). `MechCore.LegacyBalance` restores the old steps, +12% range, two hardpoints (`MechDuel`) and no battering for that A/B (not the bay's repair rate: duels never dock) (`MechDuel.Run{Full,Base}Fresh{Old,New}`); the runners reset it.
- **Gun reloads carry the remainder** (`TickGuns`: `cooldown = max(cooldown, -dt) + part.cooldown x battering`). Resetting to the full cooldown on the tick it fired lost up to half the rate of a 0.09-0.1 s weapon at the evaluation's dt 0.1; the clamp keeps a gun that waited for a target from firing a burst.
- **Parts are picked at the drop.** `BeginMatch` draws only the pilot (`Faction.design` is a placeholder until `designChosen`); at the inbound call `GameWorld.FitOutMech` runs `MechArmoury.Compose`: 12 candidates from `MechParts.Generate`, scored against `MechArmoury.Read` (enemy infantry/armour/Mech/structure value — omniscient like MechBrain; **the Commander never reads it**) as offence against that mix x toughness^0.5, less for a design with no answer to part of it or one that repeats the other side's body, then picked by weight (score/best)^3.5, so it adapts but varies. An Extra Hardpoint bought before the drop assumes a spare mount (every frame has two; Compose skips candidates with fewer; the upgrade has one level, the second spare is unused). `MechDesigns.ArmourySurvey` (200 seeds, four enemy mixes) should show ~375-385 distinct loadouts in 400, the two sides sharing a body 3-4%, a flame weapon on 67-68% of Mechs vs infantry / 47% vs armour, a laser or railgun on 71-72% / 85% (vs an enemy Mech: flame 50%, laser or railgun 83%).
- **Flame Tower** (`MechWeapon.FlameTower`, appended) is a top-mounted turret: `WeaponPart.turret`/`turnRate`, `MechGun.yaw` (relative to the torso) turned in `TickGuns`, `Aimed` and the fire cone follow it, `MechCore.Muzzle` rotates with it, `MechView` turns the kit's `Turret` group (the other way on a mirrored left-hand mount). The survey found the arm flamer overtuned against a crowd (50 Troopers on average, one at 86): it now does 0.16 to infantry in a 16-degree cone.
- **A Mech's guns take a Mech-killer first** (`MechCore.PickTarget`: anything with `vsMech` > 2 scores -22, after the enemy Mech's -25): the Mech-Hunter is meant to be easily destroyed, and ranked by distance and armour alone it was one more light target among its escort (`RosterTrials.Hunter`'s fair fight: it lived either way, that design's guns doing 112 hp in 122 shots).
- Every weapon carries three damage multipliers (`WeaponPart.vsLight/vsHeavy/vsStructure`, `Projectile.ClassMul`). **The projectile pool is shared**, so `GameWorld.Fire` resets them to 1 (a rifle round reused from a Mech's shell once kept its 0.5x against infantry).
- Models are drawn `MechCore.ModelScale` (1.2) larger than the kit; the sim's muzzle points follow.

## Presentation
- `View/MechView.cs` assembles the Mech from `Resources/MechKit` (per-part prefabs and points, `Editor/MechKitBuilder.cs`, run by Build step 2 from `Art/Models/mechs.json`), walks it by two-bone IK with planted feet, raises `MechView.Footfall`, hands it to `View/MechWreck.cs` on death. `View/FXDirector.Mech.cs` has its effects, `AudioDirector` its sounds (`make_audio.py --mech` builds only those).
- **Springs must hold at any frame length** (the evaluation runs at 8x and more): the banner's, stepped once a frame, went to NaN in one long frame, logged an error every frame after and stalled a Full evaluation for hours. They step 20 ms at a time and reset on NaN.
- **A tracked Mech rides on its tracks** (`MechView.Roll`): the hull settles onto a plane fitted through the ground along both runs (7 samples each over the flat length) through a critically damped `Spring` with rate and tilt capped (up fast, down gently), height smoothed as a world height (the agent's NavMesh height steps), then lifted so no point of either run is under the ground at the tilt it has. **A positive Euler z lifts the right side** (the roll was once given minus the slope: a tracked Mech leaned the wrong way on a side slope and sank its uphill track). The landing squat (`crouch`) drops only the torso onto the suspension. `Unit.UpdateFacing` steers a tracked Mech for `agent.steeringTarget` with a built-up turn rate (`yawVel`), not the agent's momentary velocity.
  `MechTrackRide` measures it against `MechView.LegacyTrackRide` over three routes, sampling at the **end of the frame** (a coroutine resumed after Update sees the agent moved and the hull not). Targets met: track into ground max 5 cm (was 162; >5 cm in 0% of frames, was 92%), roll rate 4.2 deg/s (7.9), reversals 0.15/s (0.68), max roll 16 deg (25), heading reversals 0.05/s (~1).
- A Mech standing inside trees fells them by footprint (`Vegetation.CrushUnderMechs`).

## Balance is measured, not guessed
`MechTrials`/`MechDuel` (`Editor/MechTrials.cs`) fight a Mech against N Maulers or Troopers, spawned 55 m off, on a flat corridor of seed 1000 and bisect for the smallest N that kills it (targets for a bare Mech, the mean over twelve designs: **6-7 Maulers, ~10 Troopers**; a fully upgraded one should stay within ~1.7x of that). **Read `killed by` (the bracket's top, the smallest group seen to kill it)**: Maulers bisect to the unit from 1-20, Troopers to two from 2-48, and the report ends with the means (`(lo+hi)/2` of a one-wide bracket floors to the largest the Mech beat; a floor of 6 Maulers once sat on the target). `RunBaseFreshAll` (twelve, both types), `RunBaseFreshAllMaulers` / `RunFullFreshMaulers` (`MaulersOnly`, half the time, for tuning passes). **Use `MechDuel.Fresh` (a map reload per fight) for any comparison:** fought one after another on one site, burnt grass, craters and felled trees skewed later fights (85 Troopers that killed an upgraded Mech in 15 s alone "lost" to it in a series). Trooper numbers move ~20% per design between runs; compare means over designs, Maulers first. The first six designs are lighter than the last six (bare 16.8 vs 24.5 Maulers): a `*Quick` six-design number is not the twelve-design mean. Numbers hold at the duel's dt 0.1 (timeScale 6); a gun fires at most once a step, so at a played frame rate the 0.09 s rotary cannon gets its full 11/s, 10% more than a duel sees. Every named `MechDuel`/`BayEdgeCases` entry point goes through `Launch`, which sets every switch (`Upgrades`, `Seeds`, `Fresh`, `LegacyBalance`, `ProbeN`; `Only`, `IgnoreSites`, `MechGateInBase`), and the report's first line names them (the bare `Run()` keeps whatever is set): a wrapper that left a switch alone once ran on the last run's settings. A run replaced by another is stopped at once (`StopAllCoroutines`) and resets nothing (the new one is current). One cut off by stopping play resets them in `OnDestroy`; a reload waits for the new scene, not a fixed time, and one that never comes up, or a scene reloaded under a BayEdgeCases run, stops it with FAILED; a match decided before the bay goes up skips the case. A duel moves back into the scene after its seed reload unless `Fresh` (left on DontDestroyOnLoad it outlived others' reloads with the AI silenced). Checked: a legacy fresh duel replaced by `RunBase` reported "one site, the new balance"; after stopping play mid-duel every switch read default. `MechDuel.ProbeTroopers85` logs a crowd every 10 s (in reach, targeting, moving, idle). `MechTrials.Watch` logs an AI-vs-AI match's bays, Mechs, upgrades, advice. `MechShowcase.Run`/`MechCombatLook.Run` photograph (`Temp/mech*.png`). Trials set the `Commander.Suspended` static: see `../../AI/CLAUDE.md`.
