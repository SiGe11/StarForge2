# Shaders and rendering constraints — area notes

Draw-side effects (smoke, pressure, wrecks): `../Scripts/View/CLAUDE.md`. URP/asset generation quirks: `../Editor/CLAUDE.md`.

- All StarForge materials use hand-written shaders here (`SF_Unit`, `SF_Terrain`, `SF_Grass`, `SF_Water`, `SF_FogOfWar`, …), not URP Lit (the animals use URP Lit: skinned, few, cheap).
- **Alpha test only on tree foliage** (`clip`; leaf cards — bark and inner canopy never clip). It disables early depth testing on Apple's tile-based GPUs and costs most of the ~1 ms the card crowns added. Blades, debris, holograms are opaque geometry. **Do not add alpha test anywhere else.**
- **No shader writes motion vectors**, so STP/TAA are not used (moving units would smear). Any URP effect whose noise is re-rolled each frame for TAA to average out boils on screen: SSAO uses interleaved-gradient noise (its default blue noise made the grass vibrate).
- **Fog-of-war pass** (URP `FullScreenPassRendererFeature` `FogOfWar` running `SF_FogOfWar`, created by `RenderSetup`) also does height fog, haze, sun scattering. Globals set by `FogOfWarRenderer` and `Atmosphere`; ground mask by `GroundMask`. Extend that pass rather than adding full-screen passes. The opaque texture is on for the water's refraction (`SF_Water` samples it); nothing else may rely on it without accounting for the copy's cost. **The copy itself measures nothing here**: with it never made (a trial switch, the camera held on a wood by the lake, scale held 0.75) 46.9 fps against 46.7 and 47.3 with it, so gating it by whether water is in view was dropped (2026-11-20; a frustum test also sees the water under the hills in front of it and never closed).
- **After adding shaders or keywords, play a match and re-run Build step 5**, or the player hitches while Metal compiles the variant. Unity's recorded list only covers what rendered since the last script reload, so step 5 merges into the saved collection; check the new shader's GUID is in it.
- **Sub-pixel detail flickers** at RTS camera distance (per-blade grass flutter did): check visual changes with a still camera using the burst capture (root `CLAUDE.md`), not only in motion.
- **SF_Unit's palette (`_UsePalette`, `_PaletteMap`)** draws a model with one material: a uniform branch, not a keyword (no new variants), reading three texels by colour G's slot index. Rows: base and smoothness; emission and metallic; wear, grime, AO, panel detail on or off. **An `[HDR]` colour reaches the shader as it was set, unconverted**, so a material's `_EmissionColor` (set as `.gamma`) is that gamma value in the shader and the palette stores the same: stored linear, the team plates lost their pale glow. Rock, crystal and ore glass need their own materials. Every SF_Unit model draws this way (`export_fbx.merge_palette`), so a slot's material only matters through `BuildPalette`: a number added to a slot goes in a palette row too, and `CheckPalette` compares them.
- **MSAA (High, Balanced = 2x)** shades a pixel from its centre even when only a sample touches the triangle; on thin geometry seen edge-on interpolants extrapolate far outside range (grass blew single pixels into HDR sparks that bloom made flashing lights). Grass interpolants are `centroid` and clamped: do the same for any new thin, instanced geometry.
- **Coplanar faces z-fight** from the RTS camera (`sf_model.ring_flat` collars were wound inside out and flush with the drum below). Check new models for same-facing coplanar faces across materials.
- Scanned rocks use `SF_Rock` (own UVs and maps), not `SF_Unit`; its shared passes are not instancing-aware, so its materials keep GPU instancing off (SRP-batched).
- `SF_Smoke`: do not rescale the atlas coverage to 1; see `../Scripts/View/CLAUDE.md` (Smoke). `SF_Flipbook` = channel-packed premultiplied flipbooks (R sun-lit smoke, G fire sqrt, B sky-lit smoke, A coverage).
- `_SF_Rain` (how wet a shower has left everything, 0..1, from `FXDirector.Weather`) is a plain global float declared outside every material CBUFFER, behind no keyword (no new variants): SF_Terrain feeds it into its waterline wetness (x0.6), SF_Unit darkens 12% and raises smoothness, SF_Grass darkens 20%, SF_Rock darkens 25% and glistens, SF_GroundDecal's tread marks take x1.35 coverage. Any new ground or unit shader should read it. `_SF_Shower` (the shower now, 0..1) is declared the same way; SF_Water damps its glint 75% and dulls the surface with it.
- **A leaf card seen edge-on fades out** (`SF_Tree` `CardFacing`, material vector `_CardGraze` 0.10..0.34 of |face normal . view|, y 0 = off; `-sfnograze` on the player): the cards face out of the crown, so from the RTS camera those on its far side and top lay along the line of sight and drew as streaks of stretched leaves round every crown. Coverage is scaled down, so the sparsest parts go first. From the face's own normal (ddx/ddy of world position: the vertex normals point out of the crown). **Every camera pass that clips a card applies it** (ForwardLit, DepthOnly, DepthNormals: or SSAO shades leaves that are not drawn); **the ShadowCaster must not** (to the sun the card is a different shape). No keyword, no new variant. Check `TreeTrials.Graze` (play mode at the menu; three stands, zoom 26 and 55, fade off and on with the wind stilled, on a copy of the material; `Temp/treelook_*.png`): 1.6-5.6% of pixels change, the streaks gone, crowns no thinner; then the same 40 frames of wind each way, pixels reversing six times or more: 0.045% off, 0.039% on at zoom 26, 0.010% / 0.007% at 55 (the fade adds no shimmer in the Editor; the player's burst is unrun). On the player, with the unit-surface change in the same pair, no cost shows: scale held at 0.75, 44.2, 43.7 fps with it against 44.1, 44.5 without (`../Scripts/View/CLAUDE.md`, Unit surfaces).
- **The meadow's ground and its grass agree through `SF_Meadow.hlsl`** (included by SF_Terrain's fragment, SF_Grass's vertex and SF_Tree): `SFMeadowPatch` (dry 0 .. lush 1, from the terrain's variation channel: the AO texture's G, which the terrain has in hand and SF_Grass reads per clump as the global `_SF_TerrainAO` that `GroundScatter` binds when it grows; **never a noise worked out per terrain pixel**: with one the build read 44.7, 44.3 fps against the previous build's 45.4, 45.4 over 420 s at a held 0.75; from the texture 44.6, 45.0 against 44.9; `../Scripts/Game/CLAUDE.md`) picks `SFTurfColor` for the terrain and `SFGrassPatchTint` for the blades. **The turf's colour is divided by the terrain's own variation factor** (0.86..1.12, applied to all albedo further down, from the same channel): left in, a dry patch's pale turf was darkened under blades the tint brightens. An in-place regeneration (trials only) does not regrow the cover, so the blades keep the old map's patches. SF_Terrain lays that turf where tufts grow by `MapGenerator.GrassChance`'s rule (meadow weight, no cliff, level, 0.3-0.9 m clear of the water), keeping the scan's light and dark and letting its stones (height over 0.55-0.85) show; the backdrop gets it too. Without it a meadow was dark spikes on brown sand and from the overview no meadow at all. Burnt ground is grey ash (80% desaturated), not the turf darkened. `_SF_TuneA`/`_SF_TuneB` are offsets on these looks, zero in every run that does not pass `-sftune` (`View/ShaderTune.cs`: `-sftune _SF_TuneA=x,y,z,w` sets any global for one player run, so a look judged in the gallery is tuned without a rebuild).
- **SF_Terrain's turf and ash take their surface from detail textures** (`_TurfLush`, `_TurfDry`, `_BurntTex`: `Art/Textures/turf_lush.jpg`, `turf_dry.jpg`, `ground_burnt.jpg` from `Tools/blender/pack_turf.py`): each is its picture over its own mean, **0.25 linear = the mean, x4 in the shader**, so `SFTurfColor` and the ash grey stay the authority on colour and a meadow seen from far off, where the detail blurs to its mean, is the colour it was (overview shot against the untextured build: meadow luminance within 0.5%). Lush is sampled only where the patch is not fully dry and dry only where it is not fully lush, the dry one on the rotated coordinates so the two never tile in step, with gradients from outside the branches. **Their strengths (`_TurfTile` z, w) are 0 until `MapBuilder` binds the textures**: the `grey` default samples 0.5 and would double the ground. With the turf texture on, the scan's own light and dark keeps 0.45 of its say. `-sftune _SF_TuneC=-1,0,-1,0` turns both off for an A/B on one build.
- **A steep face is drawn as the cliff layer whatever the splat says** (SF_Terrain, from n.y 0.78 down, all of it by 0.60; `-sftune _SF_TuneD=-1` turns it off): the splat's slope rule works on the generator's 2 m cells, so a step narrower than them can keep its meadow or gravel layer, planar XZ, on a wall (insurance: no such face has been found in a picture; a gallery pair against `_SF_TuneD=-1` that shows no difference anywhere would be the reason to take it out). Grass, flowers and the fire's fuel need n.y 0.86 or more, so nothing grows on what it turns to rock; `FXDirector.GroundAt` still takes its dust colour from `SplatAt` there (no unit stands on such a face). **It is not what the smeared face needed** (seed 1000, `-sflook 183.42,162.17,30`, rule on against off: 0.08% of pixels differ; that face was rock already), and neither was the vertex normal (a fix taking the face's normal from the position's derivatives changed nothing: the two agree there; taken out). **The streaks are the cliff scan's own stretch:** `_CliffStretch` 0.55 multiplies the height that is V on the cliff's two side planes, so the scan (`aerial_rocks_02`, slabs photographed from above: there are no strata in it) stands 1.8 times taller than wide on a wall. **Kept at 0.55 on the pictures** (`Builds/veg/look/`, 2026-11-20, old against 0.75 and 1.0 at zoom 30, 55 and 110: `pair_075.jpg`, `pair_z55.jpg`, `pair_z110.jpg`, `stretch_pair.jpg`): unstretched the close face reads as slabs and moss, but at the zooms a match is played at the stretched joints are what make a terrace read as a cliff, and at 1.0 the same walls came out 10% darker at zoom 55 (2% at 110) and muddier (another stretch also puts another part of the 16 m scan on a given wall, so one spot's brightness says little). `-sftune _SF_TuneD=0,0.45` draws it unstretched for one run (y is added to the stretch), so the look can be judged again without a build; to change it for good set `_CliffStretch` in `MapBuilder.BuildTerrainMaterial` (the material asset keeps its old float, so the shader's default alone changes nothing) and run `RefreshTerrainMaterial`. **Before blaming a projection, look at what the shader believes:** a temporary `return` of (n.y, the face's own n.y, top-plane weight), of the layer's colour alone and of a checker of its UVs settled in four shots what two fixes written from the picture had not.
- **SF_Grass has two ways in.** `_SF_GRASS_BUFFER` (a local keyword on GroundScatter's copies of the two grass materials) reads each clump from one `StructuredBuffer` by `SV_InstanceID` + `_SF_GrassBase` (a float: exact below 2^24), drawn with `Graphics.RenderMeshPrimitives`; without it the clump comes through Unity's instancing (`-sfgrassold`). In the buffer variant never use the instancing macros (the attribute struct has `SV_InstanceID` itself). **`SFGrassKeep` and `GroundScatter.KeepAt` must stay one curve**: the shader sinks the clumps late in their chunk's order over a 0.06 band past it, and the CPU draws only the instances up to that band at the chunk's nearest point; change one alone and clumps pop at the count. A blade is 5-8 cm wide and **widens across its own line with distance** (`side` in TEXCOORD1, x2.6 by 110 m): thinner up close reads as grass, sub-pixel far off shimmers. **The sun's shadow on grass is taken per vertex** (one hardware-filtered tap: the soft-shadow keywords are fragment-only), `_SF_GrassPixelShadow` 1 puts it per pixel for an A/B (at the wood, two bursts each: 1.66, 1.72% vibrating per vertex against 1.72, 1.63% per pixel, so no shimmer of its own; one 120 s probe each, 46.7-47.3 against 45.8 fps). Flower heads are opaque geometry (uv.y 2 petal, 3 eye), drawn in to nothing by 70 m. Burnt grass is short black stubble drawn in toward its root (laid flat and spread it was brown flakes).
- **Leaves are not lit through `UniversalFragmentPBR`** (SF_Tree, foliage only; bark still is): wrapped sun (0.45), a floor of 0.30 under the crown's own shadow, light through the leaf on faces turned from the sun (yellower, strongest toward the sun), sky from above mixed into the ambient, additional lights by `LIGHT_LOOP_BEGIN` (it needs the local named `inputData`). Through PBR a card turned from the sun or under another card had only the ground's bounce and every crown was black underneath; the new path also drops the reflection probe and specular lobe on the frame's most overdrawn pixels. The char and blossom noises run only on a plant that has burnt or a kind that flowers. `_InnerShade` (per kind, `VegetationRenderer`): 0.62 for foliage inside a crown of cards, 1.05 for a kind with no cards (the reeds were near black). One shadow tap on leaves instead of the pipeline's nine measured nothing (46.7 fps against 46.7, the camera on a wood) and was taken out again.
- SF_Tree/SF_Grass wind and blast pressure live in `SF_Wind.hlsl` (contract in `../Scripts/View/CLAUDE.md`, wind in `../Scripts/World/CLAUDE.md`). Foliage is two-sided (`_Cull` 0); leaf cards and canopy clusters carry exporter-made custom normals pointing out of the crown, so a crown lights as one mass (`Tools/blender/CLAUDE.md`).
