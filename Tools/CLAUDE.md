# Tools: asset fetching, packers, audio — area notes

Licence rules (root `CLAUDE.md`) apply to every fetch/pack here: verify → register in `THIRD-PARTY.md` + README credit → `python3 Tools/check_licences.py`.

## Sources and packers
CC0 only (public repo). `python3 Tools/fetch_assets.py` fetches into `Art/Source/` (git-ignored): Poly Haven ground, rock and plant scans (plants as .blend, ~700 MB: pass asset ids to fetch only some, e.g. `island_tree_01`), ambientCG leaf atlases, Kenney sound packs, Quaternius animated animals, OpenGameArt music, the recorded gunshots/tree falls/wood breaks (Free Firearm Sound Library, kheetor, AntumDeluge, rubberduck), JangaFX EmberGen VDBs (`--jangafx`). No 7z in the standard library: macOS `tar -xf` (libarchive) reads the firearm library's .7z. `afconvert -f WAVE -d LEI16@32000 -c 1 in out.wav` decodes ogg/mp3/wav for numpy work. Keep renders and staged files in the repo tree, not the scratchpad (a restart once wiped it).
Poly Pizza models are mixed licences (many CC-BY): check each. Poly Haven trees are millions of triangles, unusable directly (hence the bake).
Packers → committed assets: `blender/pack_textures.py` (terrain colour + scanned height in alpha, normals), `blender/build_rocks.py` (decimated rock FBXs + `rocks.json`, merged into `ModelFactory.Meta`), `blender/make_leaf_cards.py` (leaf-spray textures from ambientCG atlases), `pack_fauna.py`, `blender/build_songbird.py` (re-poses/re-animates the perched bird into a flying one with a palette texture), `blender/render_flipbooks.py` (EmberGen VDBs → flipbooks via Cycles; `blender/flipbooks/*.json`), `make_audio.py`, `blender/encode_audio.py` (Vorbis; macOS has no ogg encoder).
Generated textures: `make_leaf_textures.py` (inner-canopy leaf pile), `make_flame_sheet.py`, `make_smoke_puffs.py`, `make_panel_texture.py` (hull plating `armor.png`/`armor_n.png` for SF_Unit; `_n` is its height, turned into normals on import). The water keeps the original's `water_n.jpg` ripples (a synthesised replacement was rejected: "waves were bad"; its foam, glint and reflection changes stay). Blender is the image library for scripts that need one (system Python has only numpy).
App icon: `Art/AppIcon.png` from `Assets/icon.png` by `python3 Tools/make_app_icon.py`; keep Apple's 824 px rounded-square template (macOS 26 puts any other shape on a grey plate).

## Audio build (`make_audio.py`)
Copies Kenney effects and music under game names with `music.json` loudness, synthesises ambience loops, builds guns and wood (each shot mixed from a near and a far recording of the same gun with a slap-back, every take levelled to one loudness with `match()`). `--mech` builds only the Mech sounds.
- Weapons, blasts and engine beds are *built* from registered CC0 recordings: `build_weapon`/`build_blast` add a falling sub `thump()` and a `rolling()` report, then `hipass_lin(..., 45)` removes everything below 45 Hz. **The high-pass matters**: a 40 Hz sine carries enormous energy for its loudness, a laptop speaker cannot move it, and `match()` then turns the audible part down to make room (the first cannon was 95% below 120 Hz and came out a quiet thud).
- Nothing can be listened to from the agent's side. Check a new sound by the share of its energy under 120 Hz and its spectral centroid: rifle ~800 Hz, cannon ~180, engine bed ~220 (it was 83 Hz before its knock was soft-clipped and the bed high-passed), shell landing 240-320, structure 150-200.
- Music: judge a candidate with `python3 Tools/measure_music.py <file>` (key, share of time the harmony is minor, dissonant-interval/beating content, brightness, note density, swell; `--segments` catches a dark passage inside a warm track), not by its title. Aim: major key, minor_share well under 0.5, dissonance under ~0.05, swell under 3 (the calm set's first two tracks were 0.68 and 0.46 minor and sounded creepy).
